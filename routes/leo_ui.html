1Spreadsheet and request

Upload the notes, then say in words what you want, for example give seizure types for the first 100 subjects. Nothing runs yet: LEO shows what it understood and you can change it.

You can name things such as seizure type, medications, EEG, MRI, etiology, surgery; limit rows with “first N subjects”, “first N notes”, “where clinic is Neurology”, “after 2022-01-01”; and put your own questions in quotes.
