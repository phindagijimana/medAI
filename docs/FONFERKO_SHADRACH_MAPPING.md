## Practical uses of this corpus in MedAI-EPI

1. **Bootstrap few-shot examples.** Replace hand-written synthetic examples in `services/prompt_registry.py` with adapted ExECT-v2 letters + annotations. Adapted example IDs get citation provenance to the Zenodo DOIs.
2. **Schema-completeness cross-check.** The gaps table above surfaces v0.2.1 fields that need active annotator attention (they can't be back-derived).
3. **Annotator training material.** Guidelines PDF at `data/fonferko_shadrach_2024/zenodo_8382589/ExECT V2 .1- What and How of annotating_v9.docx`.
4. **External-validation slice (Flagship, §26).** Evaluate our system on the 200 letters after schema-mapping — reports out-of-institution robustness.
5. **Weak-supervision seed for B1 BioClinicalBERT.** The 200 letters + attributes can train per-field heads for medications, seizure frequency, diagnosis category.
