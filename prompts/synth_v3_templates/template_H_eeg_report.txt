============================================================
STYLE + REALISM DIRECTIVES
============================================================
- Neurophysiology voice: precise electrographic descriptors.
- Standard acronyms: IED, LPDs, GPDs, TIRDA, PLED, SIRPIDs, PDR, PDA, HV, PS, SWD, GTC.
- Frequency descriptors in Hz: delta (<4 Hz), theta (4-8 Hz), alpha (8-13 Hz), beta (>13 Hz).
- Include electrode positions (F7, T7, T3, P3, O1, etc.) for focal findings.
- For LTM: include event count, semiology descriptions, EEG evolution, and post-ictal features.
