============================================================
STYLE + REALISM DIRECTIVES
============================================================
- Voice: first person ("I", "we"), past tense for HPI, present tense for exam.
- Use US-standard physician shorthand LIBERALLY: s/p (NOT "status post"), r/o (NOT "rule out"), hx of (NOT "history of"), BID, QID, PRN, NKDA, LTM, EMU, IED, MTS, MRI, EEG, VNS, RNS, DBS, VEEG. If you catch yourself writing "status post" — replace with "s/p"; "history of" — replace with "hx of".
- Brand-then-generic drug naming when introducing a medication: "Keppra (levetiracetam) 1000 mg BID". Subsequent mentions can drop the parens.
- Deliberate imperfection: 1-3 sentence FRAGMENTS in the exam ("Alert, oriented x3. In NAD. CN II-XII intact. Motor 5/5. DTRs 2+ symmetric."). Not full sentences with subjects — real dictated exams are terse.
- Template placeholders: "No X on file", "NKDA", "Twelve systems reviewed, all others negative", "Vitals per nursing", "Pain scale: 0/10".
- Include 1-2 dictation artifacts if natural: "Dr. XXX and I discussed the case", "The patient was seen and evaluated by our APP xxx, who agrees with the assessment above."
- Do NOT include structured tables. Prose only, with occasional bulleted medication list.
- Do NOT include LOINC codes, ICD-10 codes, or CPT codes in the body text.

============================================================
MetaCAT AMBIGUATION DIRECTIVES — CRITICAL
============================================================
The semantic plan may include five optional fields. When any of them are populated, you MUST express them in the note explicitly so a downstream MetaCAT annotator can learn from the pattern. If the field is absent, do NOT invent content for it.

1. **DENIED_FINDINGS** (Presence = NEGATED): For each item, weave in a
   "denies X" or "no history of X" phrase in HPI or ROS. Example: if the
   plan says `denied_findings: ["aura", "family history of epilepsy"]`,
   include somewhere: "She denies aura preceding events. No family history
   of epilepsy." Use natural phrasing, mix positions across the note.

2. **FAMILY_HISTORY** (Experiencer = FAMILY): populate the Family History
   section with the relative + condition. Example: "Family history: mother
   with epilepsy." NOT "no family history" unless the plan specifically
   omits family history.

3. **PRIOR_ASMS** (Temporality = PAST): distinguish clearly from current.
   Use narrative like "Previously trialed X (discontinued due to Y)" or
   "In the past, failed adequate trials of X and Y." Do NOT list these
   under Current Medications.

4. **HEDGED_FINDINGS** (Certainty = PROBABLE / POSSIBLE): use hedging
   language for each — "possibly focal onset", "cannot exclude...",
   "consistent with...", "likely...", "suggestive of...". Do NOT commit
   these to definite claims.

5. **HYPOTHETICAL_TRIGGERS** (Hypothetical): weave into the Recommendations
   / Plan section. Example: "If she has another breakthrough seizure, will
   escalate to LTM."

If a Presurgical / DRE patient is described, name the specific device
being considered by BRAND when discussing options: "NeuroPace RNS System",
"Medtronic DBS Percept", "LivaNova VNS SenTiva". Do not just say "RNS" or
"DBS" without a brand at least once.
