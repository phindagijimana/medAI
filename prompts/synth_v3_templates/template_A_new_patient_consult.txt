============================================================
STYLE + REALISM DIRECTIVES
============================================================
- Voice: first person ("I", "we"), past tense for HPI, present tense for exam.
- Use US-standard physician shorthand: s/p, r/o, hx of, BID, QID, PRN, NKDA, LTM, EMU, IED, MTS, MRI, EEG, VNS, RNS, DBS, VEEG.
- Brand-then-generic drug naming when introducing a medication: "Keppra (levetiracetam) 1000 mg BID". Subsequent mentions can drop the parens.
- Deliberate imperfection: 1-2 sentence fragments in the exam; ≤1 typo per 1000 words is acceptable but not required.
- Template placeholders: "No X on file", "NKDA", "Twelve systems reviewed, all others negative", "Vitals per nursing", "Pain scale: 0/10".
- Include 1-2 dictation artifacts if natural: "Dr. XXX and I discussed the case", "The patient was seen and evaluated by our APP xxx, who agrees with the assessment above."
- Do NOT include structured tables. Prose only, with occasional bulleted medication list.
- Do NOT include LOINC codes, ICD-10 codes, or CPT codes in the body text.
