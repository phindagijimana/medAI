**Clinical Indication:** Generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures, onset at age 11, occurring approximately twice monthly. Structural etiology; drug-responsive on current topiramate therapy.

**Technique:** Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

**Findings:** Focal cortical architectural abnormality compatible with focal cortical dysplasia.

**Impression:** Focal cortical dysplasia.
