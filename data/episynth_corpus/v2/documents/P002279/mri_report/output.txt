MRI BRAIN

**Clinical History:**  
Focal impaired-awareness seizures, with onset at age 8, occurring approximately once per month. Structural etiology. Drug-responsive on current oxcarbazepine and lamotrigine therapy.

**Technique:**  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy-focused protocol.

**Findings:**  
Focal cortical dysplasia is identified.

**Impression:**  
Focal cortical dysplasia, consistent with the reported structural etiology of the patient’s focal epilepsy.
