**MRI BRAIN**

**Clinical History:**  
Focal aware seizures beginning at age 30, occurring once weekly. Structural etiology. Drug-responsive epilepsy. Current medications: perampanel and lacosamide.

**Technique:**  
Multiplanar MRI of the brain was performed.

**Findings:**  
Hippocampal sclerosis.

**Impression:**  
Hippocampal sclerosis.
