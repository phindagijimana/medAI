CLINICAL INDICATION: Drug-resistant focal epilepsy of structural etiology, characterized by focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 29 and occurring approximately 5 times per month. Current antiseizure medications include levetiracetam, topiramate, and brivaracetam.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Focal abnormality of cortical development with imaging features consistent with focal cortical dysplasia.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia.
