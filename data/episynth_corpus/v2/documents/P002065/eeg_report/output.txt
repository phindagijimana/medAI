Clinical Indication  
History of combined generalized and focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 28, occurring approximately once per year. Etiology is unknown. Seizures are drug responsive. Current antiseizure medication is zonisamide.

Technique  
Routine digital scalp EEG was recorded using electrodes placed according to the International 10–20 system.

Findings  
The interictal recording demonstrates both generalized and focal epileptiform abnormalities.

Impression  
Abnormal routine EEG due to the presence of generalized and focal epileptiform abnormalities. These findings indicate both generalized and focal epileptogenic potential; seizure classification and localization require clinical correlation.
