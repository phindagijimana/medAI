CLINICAL INDICATION  
Routine EEG in a patient with combined generalized and focal epilepsy, with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 34 and occurring approximately 3 times per month. Etiology is genetic, associated with an STXBP1 c.902A>G variant. Seizures are drug responsive. Current antiseizure medication is carbamazepine.

TECHNIQUE  
Routine scalp EEG recording using standard electrode placement.

FINDINGS  
No electrographic findings were provided for interpretation. Background organization, activation responses, and the presence or absence of interictal epileptiform discharges or electrographic seizures therefore cannot be characterized.

IMPRESSION  
Indeterminate routine EEG due to unavailable electrographic findings. No EEG-based inference regarding seizure type or localization can be made.
