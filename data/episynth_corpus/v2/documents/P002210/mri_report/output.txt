MRI BRAIN

**Clinical History:** Focal-onset aware seizures beginning at age 52, occurring approximately 6 times per week. Structural etiology. Drug-responsive epilepsy. Current antiseizure medications: carbamazepine and valproate.

**Technique:** Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy-focused protocol.

**Findings:** Focal cortical dysplasia is identified.

**Impression:** Focal cortical dysplasia.
