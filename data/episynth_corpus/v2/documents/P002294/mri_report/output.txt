**Technique:**  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using a dedicated epilepsy protocol.

**Findings:**  
Focal cortical dysplasia, Palmini type IIb.

**Impression:**  
Focal cortical dysplasia, Palmini type IIb, representing a structural substrate for the patient’s focal impaired-awareness seizures. The patient has drug-responsive focal epilepsy with onset at age 26 and approximately two seizures per year. Current antiseizure medication: levetiracetam.
