**MRI BRAIN**

**Clinical history:** Focal epilepsy with focal to bilateral tonic-clonic seizures, onset at age 8, occurring approximately once weekly. Structural etiology. Drug-responsive on current lacosamide and valproate.

**Technique:** Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed.

**Findings:** Encephalomalacia is present.

**Impression:** Encephalomalacia.
