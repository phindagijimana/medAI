**MRI BRAIN (EPILEPSY PROTOCOL)**

**Clinical History:** Focal epilepsy with focal-to-bilateral tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 11, occurring approximately 5 times per month. Structural etiology. Drug-responsive. Current antiseizure medication: lamotrigine.

**Technique:** Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy-focused protocol.

**Findings:** Focal cortical dysplasia is identified.

**Impression:** Focal cortical dysplasia, representing the reported structural substrate for the patient’s focal epilepsy.
