**Clinical History:** Focal epilepsy with focal-to-bilateral tonic-clonic seizures, onset at age 77, occurring approximately 3 times per year. Structural etiology. Drug-responsive on current antiseizure medications including zonisamide and levetiracetam.

**Technique:** Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

**Findings:** Hippocampal sclerosis.

**Impression:** Hippocampal sclerosis.
