CLINICAL INDICATION: Drug-responsive focal epilepsy of structural etiology, with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 6 and occurring approximately once per month. Current antiseizure medications include zonisamide and perampanel.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Focal abnormal cortical morphology with blurring of the gray-white matter junction, compatible with focal cortical dysplasia.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, providing a structural substrate for focal epilepsy.
