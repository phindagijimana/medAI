CLINICAL HISTORY: Focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 13, occurring approximately 3 times per year. Structural etiology. Drug-responsive on current levetiracetam therapy.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical dysplasia is identified.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, consistent with the reported structural etiology of focal epilepsy.
