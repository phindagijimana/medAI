CLINICAL HISTORY: Generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 13, occurring approximately once weekly. Structural etiology. Drug-responsive on current valproate and lacosamide therapy.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical dysplasia with imaging features of Palmini type IIb, including cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white matter junction, and associated transmantle signal abnormality.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, Palmini type IIb.
