**MRI BRAIN**

**Clinical History:**  
Drug-resistant focal epilepsy with impaired awareness, onset at age 8, occurring approximately 3 times per week. Structural etiology. Current antiseizure medications: levetiracetam and brivaracetam.

**Technique:**  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy-focused protocol.

**Findings:**  
Hippocampal sclerosis is identified.

**Impression:**  
Hippocampal sclerosis.
