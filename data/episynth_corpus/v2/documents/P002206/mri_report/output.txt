**Clinical History:** Structural focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 34, occurring approximately 3 times per year. Drug-resistant. Current antiseizure medications: zonisamide, perampanel, and valproate.

**Technique:** Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy-focused protocol.

**Findings:** Hippocampal sclerosis.
