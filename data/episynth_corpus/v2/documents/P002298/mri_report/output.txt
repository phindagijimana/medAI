**MRI BRAIN**

**Clinical History:**  
Focal impaired-awareness seizures, onset at age 67, occurring approximately 5 times per month. Drug-resistant epilepsy with structural etiology. Current antiseizure medications: perampanel and levetiracetam.

**Technique:**  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy-focused protocol.

**Findings:**  
Hippocampal sclerosis.

**Impression:**  
Hippocampal sclerosis, consistent with a structural substrate for the patient’s focal epilepsy.
