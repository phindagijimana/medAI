MRI BRAIN

**Clinical history:** Generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 33, occurring approximately twice per year. Structural etiology. Drug-responsive on current perampanel therapy.

**Technique:** Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed.

**Findings:** Hippocampal sclerosis is identified. No additional imaging findings are reported.

**Impression:** Hippocampal sclerosis.
