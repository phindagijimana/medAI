**MRI BRAIN**

**Clinical History:**  
Focal aware seizures beginning at age 35, occurring approximately 4 times per month. Structural epilepsy, currently drug-responsive. Current antiseizure medications: valproate and zonisamide.

**Technique:**  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy-focused protocol.

**Findings:**  
Hippocampal sclerosis.

**Impression:**  
Hippocampal sclerosis, consistent with a structural substrate for the reported focal aware seizures.
