CLINICAL HISTORY: Drug-responsive focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures, onset at age 9 years and occurring approximately 4 times per month. Structural etiology. Current antiseizure medication: perampanel.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Hippocampal volume loss, increased T2/FLAIR signal, and loss of normal internal architecture are consistent with hippocampal sclerosis.

IMPRESSION: Hippocampal sclerosis.
