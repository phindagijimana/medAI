TECHNIQUE  
Clinical history: Focal epilepsy of structural etiology, onset at age 65, with focal-to-bilateral tonic-clonic seizures occurring approximately once monthly. Seizures are drug responsive. Current antiseizure medications are perampanel and carbamazepine. Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS  
Focal cortical dysplasia is identified.

IMPRESSION  
Focal cortical dysplasia, providing a structural substrate for the reported focal epilepsy.
