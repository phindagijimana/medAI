**Clinical Indication:** Drug-responsive focal epilepsy of structural etiology, with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 10 and occurring approximately 5 times per month. Current antiseizure medications include levetiracetam and oxcarbazepine.

**Technique:** Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed.

**Findings:** Encephalomalacia is present.

**Impression:** Encephalomalacia.
