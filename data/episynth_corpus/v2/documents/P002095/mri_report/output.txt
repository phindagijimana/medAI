Technique

Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed.

Findings

An incidental intracranial lesion is present. No further characterization is provided by the authorized imaging information.

Clinical history of metabolic focal epilepsy with focal-to-bilateral tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 12. Epilepsy is drug responsive, with current seizure frequency of 0 per month (seizure free) on lamotrigine and zonisamide.

Impression
