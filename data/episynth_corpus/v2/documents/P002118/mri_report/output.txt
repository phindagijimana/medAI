CLINICAL HISTORY: Focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures since age 5, occurring approximately twice monthly. Structural etiology. Seizures are drug responsive on current oxcarbazepine therapy.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical dysplasia is identified, characterized as Palmini type IIA.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, Palmini type IIA, providing a structural substrate for the patient’s focal epilepsy.
