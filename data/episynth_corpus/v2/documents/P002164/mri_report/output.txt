Technique

Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

Findings

Hippocampal sclerosis is present.

Impression

Hippocampal sclerosis.

Clinical history: Structural focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 75, occurring approximately three times per month. Seizures are drug-responsive. Current antiseizure medications are perampanel and lacosamide.
