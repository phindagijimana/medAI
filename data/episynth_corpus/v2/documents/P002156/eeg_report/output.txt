CLINICAL INDICATION  
History of focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 33, occurring approximately once weekly. Etiology is unknown. Seizures are drug responsive; current antiseizure medication is carbamazepine.

TECHNIQUE  
Routine scalp EEG was recorded using the international 10–20 electrode placement system.

FINDINGS  
No interictal epileptiform discharges or electrographic seizures were identified during this recording.

IMPRESSION  
No epileptiform abnormalities or electrographic seizures were recorded. This routine EEG does not independently establish seizure type or ictal localization, and the absence of epileptiform discharges does not exclude epilepsy.
