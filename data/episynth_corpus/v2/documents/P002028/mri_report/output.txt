**Technique:** Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

**Findings:** Clinical history of structural generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 11, occurring approximately five times per week. Epilepsy is drug-responsive; current antiseizure medication is levetiracetam. MRI demonstrates hippocampal sclerosis.

**Impression:** Hippocampal sclerosis.
