CLINICAL HISTORY: Drug-resistant focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures, onset at age 14. Structural etiology. Currently seizure-free (0 seizures per month) on lamotrigine and levetiracetam.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar multisequence MRI of the brain was performed.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical developmental abnormality consistent with focal cortical dysplasia.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, providing a structural substrate for the known focal epilepsy.
