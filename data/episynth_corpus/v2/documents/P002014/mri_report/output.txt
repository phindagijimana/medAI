CLINICAL HISTORY: Focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 68, occurring approximately four times per week. Structural etiology. Drug-responsive on current lamotrigine therapy.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical dysplasia is identified.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, providing a structural substrate for the reported focal epilepsy.
