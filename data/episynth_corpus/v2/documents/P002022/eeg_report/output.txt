Clinical Indication  
Drug-resistant combined generalized and focal epilepsy of unknown etiology, with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 27 and occurring approximately once per month. Current antiseizure medications are zonisamide, lacosamide, and perampanel.

Technique  
Routine scalp EEG was recorded using the international 10–20 electrode placement system with standard referential and bipolar montages.

Findings  
The recording demonstrates both generalized and focal epileptiform abnormalities. No further localization or ictal characterization is made.

Impression  
Abnormal routine EEG with combined generalized and focal epileptiform features, compatible with the reported epilepsy classification. The focal impaired-awareness seizure type is based on the clinical history and is not independently localized by this EEG.
