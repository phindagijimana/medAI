**Clinical Indication:** Structural focal epilepsy with focal aware seizures beginning at age 13, occurring approximately 5 times per month. Drug-responsive on current oxcarbazepine therapy.

**Technique:** Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

**Findings:** Hippocampal abnormality consistent with hippocampal sclerosis.

**Impression:** Hippocampal sclerosis.
