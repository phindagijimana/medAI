CLINICAL HISTORY: Drug-responsive structural focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures, onset at age 5 years and current frequency of approximately one seizure per year. Current antiseizure medication: valproate.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical thickening and blurring of the gray-white matter junction, compatible with focal cortical dysplasia. The appearance corresponds to the reported Palmini type IIA focal cortical dysplasia.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, Palmini type IIA.
