Clinical History:  
Focal aware seizures beginning at age 11, occurring approximately 2 times per week. Structural etiology. Drug-responsive epilepsy. Current antiseizure medications: oxcarbazepine and levetiracetam.

Technique:  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

Findings:  
Hippocampal sclerosis.

Impression:  
Hippocampal sclerosis, corresponding to the reported structural substrate for focal epilepsy.
