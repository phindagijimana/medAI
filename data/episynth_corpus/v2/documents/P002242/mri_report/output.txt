**MRI BRAIN**

**Clinical History:**  
Focal impaired-awareness seizures, with onset at age 6 years, occurring approximately 6 times per week. Structural etiology. Drug-responsive epilepsy. Current antiseizure medications: oxcarbazepine and topiramate.

**Technique:**  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy-focused protocol.

**Findings:**  
Focal cortical dysplasia, Palmini type IIA.

**Impression:**  
Focal cortical dysplasia, Palmini type IIA, corresponding to the reported structural etiology of the patient’s focal epilepsy.
