CLINICAL HISTORY: Focal epilepsy with focal aware seizures, onset at age 29 years, occurring approximately 4 times per month. Structural etiology. Drug-responsive on current levetiracetam therapy.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed with dedicated temporal lobe imaging.

FINDINGS: Hippocampal volume loss and signal abnormality consistent with hippocampal sclerosis.

IMPRESSION: Hippocampal sclerosis.
