CLINICAL HISTORY: Focal epilepsy with focal aware seizures, onset at age 35 years, occurring approximately twice per month. Structural etiology. Drug responsive on current levetiracetam therapy.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Imaging features of mild malformation of cortical development with oligodendroglial hyperplasia in epilepsy (MOGHE).

IMPRESSION: Mild malformation of cortical development with oligodendroglial hyperplasia in epilepsy (MOGHE), a structural epileptogenic abnormality.
