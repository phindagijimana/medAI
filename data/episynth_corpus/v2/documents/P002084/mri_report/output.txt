CLINICAL INDICATION: Drug-responsive focal epilepsy of structural etiology, with focal to bilateral tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 36. Currently seizure-free (0 seizures per month) on lamotrigine and oxcarbazepine.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical malformation with an imaging appearance consistent with focal cortical dysplasia.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, providing a structural substrate for the patient’s focal epilepsy.
