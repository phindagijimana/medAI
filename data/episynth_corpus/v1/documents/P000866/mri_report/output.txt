**Technique:**  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol, including dedicated temporal lobe imaging.

**Findings:**  
Imaging features of hippocampal sclerosis are present.

**Impression:**  
Hippocampal sclerosis.

Clinical history: Drug-responsive focal epilepsy of structural etiology, with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 16 and occurring approximately once per year. Current antiseizure medications are zonisamide and oxcarbazepine.
