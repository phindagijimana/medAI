**Technique:**  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

**Findings:**  
Focal cortical dysplasia is identified.

**Impression:**  
Focal cortical dysplasia, providing a structural substrate for the patient’s drug-resistant focal epilepsy. Clinical history includes focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 10, currently occurring approximately three times per month despite treatment with topiramate and perampanel.
