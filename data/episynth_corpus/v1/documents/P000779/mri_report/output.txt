TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol. Clinical history: drug-responsive focal epilepsy of structural etiology, with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 8 and occurring approximately once per month. Current antiseizure medications are oxcarbazepine and lamotrigine.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical abnormality with features of focal cortical dysplasia.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, providing a structural substrate for the patient’s focal epilepsy.
