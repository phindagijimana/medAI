TECHNIQUE  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS  
Clinical history of structural focal epilepsy with focal-to-bilateral tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 22, occurring approximately 6 times per week. Epilepsy is drug-resistant despite current treatment with zonisamide and carbamazepine.

A focal cortical malformation compatible with focal cortical dysplasia is identified.

IMPRESSION  
Focal cortical dysplasia.
