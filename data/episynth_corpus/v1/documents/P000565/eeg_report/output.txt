Clinical Indication:
History of combined generalized and focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 28. Seizures occur approximately once per year and are drug responsive. Etiology is unknown. Current antiseizure medication is zonisamide.

Technique:
Routine digital scalp EEG was recorded using electrodes placed according to the International 10–20 system. The recording was reviewed using standard referential and bipolar montages.

Findings:
The waking background is organized, symmetric, and reactive, without focal slowing. No focal or generalized epileptiform discharges or electrographic seizures are identified.

Impression:
Normal routine awake EEG. No epileptiform abnormalities or electrographic seizures are seen. A normal routine EEG does not exclude epilepsy.
