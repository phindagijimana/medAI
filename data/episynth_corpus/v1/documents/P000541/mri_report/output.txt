TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed using an epilepsy protocol. Clinical history of drug-resistant focal epilepsy of structural etiology, with focal to bilateral tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 25 and occurring approximately 7 times per week. Current antiseizure medications include topiramate, brivaracetam, carbamazepine, and zonisamide.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical dysplasia is identified.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, providing a structural substrate for the patient’s focal epilepsy.
