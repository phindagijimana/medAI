TECHNIQUE  
Routine digital scalp EEG was recorded using electrodes placed according to the International 10–20 system.

FINDINGS  
Interictal epileptiform abnormalities are present in both generalized and focal distributions. No further focal localization or ictal localization is assigned from this recording.

IMPRESSION  
Abnormal routine EEG demonstrating combined generalized and focal epileptiform activity. These findings are compatible with the reported epilepsy classification in the appropriate clinical context; EEG findings alone do not determine the clinical seizure type or establish an ictal onset location.
