Clinical Indication  
Drug-resistant generalized epilepsy of metabolic etiology, with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 10 and occurring approximately once per week. Current antiseizure medications are lamotrigine and zonisamide.

Technique  
Routine scalp EEG performed using the international 10–20 electrode placement system.

Findings  
No electrographic findings were provided for interpretation. Background activity, interictal abnormalities, and ictal activity therefore cannot be characterized.

Impression  
Indeterminate routine EEG because electrographic data were not provided. No EEG-based diagnosis or localization can be made.
