Clinical Indication:
History of generalized epilepsy with typical absence seizures beginning at age 6, currently occurring approximately 7 times per week. Etiology is unknown. Seizures are drug-responsive. Current antiseizure medication is lamotrigine.

Technique:
Routine scalp EEG was recorded using the international 10–20 system.

Findings:
No epileptiform discharges or electrographic seizures were recorded.

Impression:
No epileptiform abnormality or electrographic seizure was captured during this routine EEG. The absence of epileptiform activity on a routine recording does not exclude epilepsy.
