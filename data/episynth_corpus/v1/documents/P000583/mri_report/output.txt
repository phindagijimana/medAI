**Technique:** Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

**Findings:**  
Focal cortical dysplasia is identified.

Clinical history is notable for generalized myoclonic seizures beginning at age 72, occurring approximately once yearly, with a structural etiology. Seizures are drug responsive on current topiramate and oxcarbazepine therapy.

**Impression:**  
Focal cortical dysplasia.
