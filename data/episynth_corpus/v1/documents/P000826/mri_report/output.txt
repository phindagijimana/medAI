TECHNIQUE  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed.

CLINICAL HISTORY  
Drug-resistant generalized epilepsy of structural etiology, with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 5 and occurring approximately 3 times per month. Current antiseizure medications are topiramate, valproate, and lacosamide.

FINDINGS  
Imaging features of mild malformation of cortical development with oligodendroglial hyperplasia in epilepsy (MOGHE) are present.

IMPRESSION  
Mild malformation of cortical development with oligodendroglial hyperplasia in epilepsy (MOGHE).
