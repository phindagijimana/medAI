TECHNIQUE  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed.

FINDINGS  
Clinical history of drug-responsive focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures, onset at age 10 and current frequency of approximately 5 seizures per month. Reported etiology is structural. Current antiseizure medications are levetiracetam and oxcarbazepine.

Encephalomalacia is present.

IMPRESSION  
Encephalomalacia, compatible with the reported structural etiology of focal epilepsy.
