CLINICAL INDICATION: Focal epilepsy with focal-to-bilateral tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 23, attributed to a structural etiology. Drug-responsive and currently seizure-free (0 seizures per month) on lacosamide and zonisamide.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Findings are consistent with focal cortical dysplasia.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, providing a structural substrate for the patient’s focal epilepsy.
