TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol. Clinical history of drug-responsive focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures, onset at age 29, attributed to a structural etiology. Currently seizure-free (0 seizures/month) on lamotrigine and valproate.
