CLINICAL INDICATION: Drug-responsive focal epilepsy of structural etiology, with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 28 years and occurring approximately 3 times per year. Current antiseizure medications: lacosamide and levetiracetam.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical malformation with imaging features consistent with focal cortical dysplasia.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia.
