CLINICAL INDICATION: Focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 29, occurring approximately twice yearly. Structural etiology; drug responsive. Current antiseizure medications: zonisamide and oxcarbazepine.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed using an epilepsy protocol, including high-resolution coronal imaging through the temporal lobes.

FINDINGS: Hippocampal volume loss, increased T2/FLAIR signal, and loss of normal internal architecture are present, consistent with hippocampal sclerosis.

IMPRESSION: Hippocampal sclerosis.
