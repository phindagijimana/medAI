Clinical Indication:
Routine EEG in a patient with drug-responsive focal epilepsy characterized by focal impaired-awareness seizures. Seizure onset was at age 9 years, with a current frequency of approximately two seizures per year. Etiology is unknown. Current antiseizure medication is lamotrigine.

Technique:
Routine digital scalp EEG was performed using electrodes placed according to the international 10–20 system, with review in standard referential and bipolar montages.

Findings:
The background is organized and symmetric. No focal slowing, interictal epileptiform discharges, or electrographic seizures are identified.

Impression:
Normal routine EEG. No epileptiform abnormalities or electrographic seizures are identified. A normal routine EEG does not exclude a clinical diagnosis of epilepsy.
