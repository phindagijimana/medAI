Clinical Indication

Technique  
Routine digital scalp EEG recorded using electrodes placed according to the International 10–20 system and reviewed in standard montages.

Findings  
No EEG waveform findings were provided for interpretation. Accordingly, background organization and the presence or absence of interictal epileptiform discharges or electrographic seizures cannot be assessed.

Impression  
Indeterminate routine EEG due to unavailable electrographic findings. No EEG-based conclusions regarding epileptiform abnormalities or ictal localization can be made.
