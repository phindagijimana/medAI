CLINICAL INDICATION: Structural focal epilepsy with focal impaired awareness seizures beginning at age 6, occurring approximately once monthly. Drug-responsive on current zonisamide and perampanel therapy.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical malformation is present, with imaging features consistent with focal cortical dysplasia.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, providing a structural substrate for focal epilepsy.
