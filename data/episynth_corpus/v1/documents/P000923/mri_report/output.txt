CLINICAL HISTORY: Focal epilepsy with focal-to-bilateral tonic-clonic seizures, onset at age 10, occurring approximately four times per month. Structural etiology; drug-responsive. Current antiseizure medications are levetiracetam and lacosamide.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical dysplasia is identified, with appearance consistent with Palmini type IIA.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, Palmini type IIA, providing a structural substrate for the patient’s focal epilepsy.
