CLINICAL INDICATION: Structural focal epilepsy with focal aware seizures beginning at age 8, occurring approximately 3 times per month. Drug-responsive on current zonisamide therapy.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical dysplasia is identified.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, compatible with the reported structural etiology of focal epilepsy.
