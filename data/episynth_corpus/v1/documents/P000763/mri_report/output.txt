CLINICAL HISTORY  
Focal epilepsy with focal to bilateral tonic-clonic seizures, onset at age 11 years, occurring approximately 5 times per month. Structural etiology. Drug-responsive on current lamotrigine therapy.

TECHNIQUE  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS  
Focal cortical malformation consistent with focal cortical dysplasia.

IMPRESSION  
Focal cortical dysplasia.
