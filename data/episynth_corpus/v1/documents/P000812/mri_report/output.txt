CLINICAL INDICATION: Focal epilepsy with focal aware seizures beginning at age 56, attributed to a structural etiology. Drug-responsive and currently seizure-free (0 seizures per month) on brivaracetam and levetiracetam.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical architectural abnormality with cortical thickening and blurring of the gray-white matter junction, consistent with focal cortical dysplasia.

IMPRESSION: MRI findings consistent with focal cortical dysplasia, providing a structural substrate for focal epilepsy.
