Clinical Indication  
History of generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 30 and occurring approximately once per month. Etiology is categorized as immune. Seizures are drug-responsive. Current antiseizure medication is lacosamide.

Technique  
Routine scalp EEG was recorded using the international 10–20 system with standard bipolar and referential montages.

Findings  
No interictal epileptiform discharges or electrographic seizures were identified during this recording.

Impression  
Routine EEG without epileptiform abnormalities or electrographic seizures. This recording does not independently establish seizure type or ictal localization; correlation with the clinical history is required.
