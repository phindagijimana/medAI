**Technique:**  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using a dedicated epilepsy protocol with focused imaging of the temporal lobes.

**Findings:**  
Hippocampal abnormality consistent with hippocampal sclerosis.

Clinical history is of structural focal epilepsy with focal aware seizures beginning at age 27, currently occurring approximately 5 times per week. Seizures are drug responsive, with current lamotrigine therapy.

**Impression:**  
Hippocampal sclerosis, providing a structural substrate for the reported focal epilepsy.
