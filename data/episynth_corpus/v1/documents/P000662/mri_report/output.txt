CLINICAL HISTORY: Generalized epilepsy with typical absence seizures, onset at age 23. Structural etiology; drug-responsive and currently seizure-free (0 seizures per month) on zonisamide and perampanel.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Subtle cortical-subcortical abnormality characterized by blurring of the gray-white matter junction and adjacent subcortical T2/FLAIR signal alteration, demonstrating an imaging pattern compatible with mild malformation of cortical development with oligodendroglial hyperplasia in epilepsy (MOGHE).

IMPRESSION: MRI features compatible with MOGHE.
