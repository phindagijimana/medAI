CLINICAL INDICATION: Drug-resistant focal epilepsy of structural etiology, with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 7 years and occurring approximately once yearly. Current antiseizure medications: valproate, brivaracetam, oxcarbazepine, and carbamazepine.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: A focal area of cortical malformation is identified, consistent with focal cortical dysplasia.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, providing a structural substrate for focal epilepsy.
