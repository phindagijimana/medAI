**Technique:**  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

**Findings:**  
Hippocampal sclerosis is present.

**Impression:**  
Hippocampal sclerosis, providing a structural substrate for the patient’s focal epilepsy. Clinical history includes focal aware seizures beginning at age 13, occurring approximately five times per month. Epilepsy is drug responsive; current antiseizure medication is oxcarbazepine.
