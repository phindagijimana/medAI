CLINICAL HISTORY: Focal epilepsy of structural etiology with focal impaired-awareness seizures, onset at age 26 years, occurring approximately twice yearly. Drug-responsive on current levetiracetam therapy.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using a dedicated epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical thickening and abnormal cortical signal with blurring of the gray-white matter junction and associated transmantle signal abnormality, consistent with focal cortical dysplasia, Palmini type IIb.

IMPRESSION: MRI findings consistent with focal cortical dysplasia, Palmini type IIb.
