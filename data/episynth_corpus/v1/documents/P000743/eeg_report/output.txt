Clinical Indication  
Routine EEG in a patient with drug-responsive generalized epilepsy of genetic etiology due to a KCNQ2 c.881C>T variant. Typical absence seizures began at age 22 and currently occur approximately 7 times per week. Current antiseizure medications are valproate and zonisamide.

Technique  
Routine scalp EEG recording.

Findings  
No EEG waveform findings were provided; therefore, the background, interictal activity, and ictal activity cannot be characterized.

Impression  
Indeterminate routine EEG due to unavailable waveform findings. The generalized epilepsy and typical absence seizure classifications are based on the authorized clinical history and are not inferred from this EEG.
