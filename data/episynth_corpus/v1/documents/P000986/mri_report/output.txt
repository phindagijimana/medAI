**Technique:**  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

**Findings:**  
Hippocampal sclerosis is present.

Clinical history is focal epilepsy with focal-to-bilateral tonic-clonic seizures, onset at age 39, occurring approximately twice monthly. The epilepsy is categorized as structural and is drug responsive. Current antiseizure medications are zonisamide and carbamazepine.

**Impression:**  
Hippocampal sclerosis.
