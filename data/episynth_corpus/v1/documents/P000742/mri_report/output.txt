CLINICAL HISTORY: Focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 6, occurring approximately 6 times per week. Structural etiology. Drug-responsive on current oxcarbazepine and topiramate therapy.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical dysplasia with imaging features compatible with Palmini type IIa.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, compatible with Palmini type IIa, providing a structural substrate for focal epilepsy.
