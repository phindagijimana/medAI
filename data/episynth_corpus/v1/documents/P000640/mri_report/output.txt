CLINICAL HISTORY: Drug-resistant focal epilepsy of structural etiology, with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 66 and occurring approximately twice yearly. Current antiseizure medications: topiramate and zonisamide.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical dysplasia is present, with focal cortical architectural abnormality and blurring of the gray-white matter junction.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, providing a structural substrate for focal epilepsy.
