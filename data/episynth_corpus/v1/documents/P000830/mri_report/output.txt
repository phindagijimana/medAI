**Technique:**  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

**Findings:**  
Clinical history of structural generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 29, currently occurring approximately 6 times per week despite lamotrigine and perampanel, consistent with drug-resistant epilepsy. MRI demonstrates focal cortical dysplasia with an imaging phenotype consistent with Palmini type IIb.

**Impression:**  
Focal cortical dysplasia, imaging features consistent with Palmini type IIb.
