**Technique:**  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

**Findings:**  
Hippocampal volume loss and T2/FLAIR signal abnormality consistent with hippocampal sclerosis.

Clinical history of structural generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 7. Epilepsy is drug-responsive, with current seizure freedom (0 seizures per month) on lacosamide.

**Impression:**  
Hippocampal sclerosis.
