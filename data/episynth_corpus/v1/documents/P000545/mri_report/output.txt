CLINICAL HISTORY: Focal epilepsy of structural etiology, onset at age 65, with focal to bilateral tonic-clonic seizures occurring approximately once per month. Drug-responsive on current perampanel and carbamazepine therapy.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical dysplasia is identified.

IMPRESSION: Focal cortical dysplasia, providing a structural substrate for focal epilepsy.
