CLINICAL INDICATION: Structural focal epilepsy with focal impaired awareness seizures beginning at age 27, occurring approximately twice yearly. Drug-responsive on current oxcarbazepine therapy.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed.
