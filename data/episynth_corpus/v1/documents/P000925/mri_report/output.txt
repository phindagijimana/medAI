TECHNIQUE  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS  
Hippocampal sclerosis is present.

Clinical history: Structural focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 29, currently occurring approximately 5 times per month. Seizures are drug responsive. Current antiseizure medications are brivaracetam and valproate.

IMPRESSION  
Hippocampal sclerosis, providing a structural substrate for the reported focal epilepsy.
