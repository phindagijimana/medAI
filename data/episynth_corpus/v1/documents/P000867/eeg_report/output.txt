**Clinical Indication:**  
History of combined generalized and focal epilepsy of immune etiology, with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 32 and occurring approximately once per month. The epilepsy is drug responsive. Current antiseizure medications are valproate and oxcarbazepine.

**Technique:**  
Routine digital scalp EEG was performed using electrodes placed according to the International 10–20 system, with standard referential and bipolar montages.

**Findings:**  
Epileptiform abnormalities with both generalized and focal features are present. The focal abnormalities are not further localized in this report.

**Impression:**  
Abnormal routine EEG demonstrating combined generalized and focal epileptiform abnormalities. EEG findings alone do not establish the clinical seizure type or ictal onset localization.
