**Clinical History:** Drug-responsive focal epilepsy of structural etiology, with focal to bilateral tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 8 and occurring approximately once weekly. Current antiseizure medications are lacosamide and valproate.

**Technique:** Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed.

**Findings:** Encephalomalacia is present.

**Impression:** Encephalomalacia.
