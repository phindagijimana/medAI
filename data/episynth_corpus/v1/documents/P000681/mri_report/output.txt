CLINICAL INDICATION: Focal epilepsy with focal aware seizures beginning at age 35, occurring approximately twice per month. Structural etiology; drug-responsive on current levetiracetam therapy.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Focal cortical-subcortical abnormality with MRI features characteristic of mild malformation of cortical development with oligodendroglial hyperplasia in epilepsy (MOGHE).

IMPRESSION: MRI findings consistent with MOGHE.
