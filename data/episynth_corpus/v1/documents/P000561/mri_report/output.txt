TECHNIQUE  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed. Clinical history: drug-resistant focal epilepsy with focal aware seizures beginning at age 10, occurring approximately twice per year, with a structural etiology. Current antiseizure medications are lamotrigine, perampanel, and oxcarbazepine.

FINDINGS  
Intracranial vascular malformation is identified.

IMPRESSION  
Intracranial vascular malformation, providing a structural substrate for the patient’s focal epilepsy.
