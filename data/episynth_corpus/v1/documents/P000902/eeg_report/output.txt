Clinical Indication:
History of generalized tonic-clonic seizures with onset at age 70, occurring approximately twice per week in the setting of a metabolic etiology. Seizures are drug-responsive. Current antiseizure medication is brivaracetam.

Technique:
Routine scalp EEG was performed using electrodes placed according to the international 10–20 system. Digital EEG was reviewed using standard referential and bipolar montages.

Findings:
The recording demonstrates both generalized and focal epileptiform abnormalities.

Impression:
Abnormal routine EEG due to combined generalized and focal epileptiform abnormalities. These findings indicate both generalized and focal epileptogenic potential. Seizure type and ictal localization cannot be determined from this interictal recording alone.
