CLINICAL HISTORY: Structural focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures, onset at age 61, occurring approximately three times per year. Drug-resistant despite current lamotrigine, perampanel, valproate, and levetiracetam therapy.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol.

FINDINGS: Hippocampal volume loss and signal abnormality consistent with hippocampal sclerosis.

IMPRESSION: Hippocampal sclerosis.
