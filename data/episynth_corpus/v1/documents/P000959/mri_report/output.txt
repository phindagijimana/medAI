**Technique:**  
Multiplanar multisequence MRI of the brain was performed using an epilepsy protocol.

**Findings:**  
Clinical history of structural generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 30. The epilepsy is drug responsive, with current seizure freedom (0 seizures per month) on oxcarbazepine and perampanel. MRI demonstrates hippocampal sclerosis.

**Impression:**  
Hippocampal sclerosis.
