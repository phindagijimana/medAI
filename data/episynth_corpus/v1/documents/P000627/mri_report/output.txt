TECHNIQUE  
Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed using an epilepsy protocol. Clinical history: generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 13, occurring approximately once weekly. Seizures are drug responsive. Current antiseizure medications are valproate and lacosamide.

FINDINGS  
Focal cortical dysplasia with imaging features corresponding to Palmini type IIb.

IMPRESSION  
Focal cortical dysplasia, Palmini type IIb, consistent with the reported structural etiology of epilepsy.
