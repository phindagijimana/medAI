Clinical Indication:
Routine EEG in a patient with focal epilepsy characterized by focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 15, occurring approximately 3 times per year. Etiology is unknown. Seizures are drug-responsive. Current antiseizure medications are perampanel and valproate.

Technique:
Routine digital scalp EEG was recorded using standard electrode placement.

Findings:
No EEG recording findings were provided for interpretation. Background organization, interictal epileptiform activity, and ictal activity therefore cannot be characterized.

Impression:
Indeterminate routine EEG due to unavailable electrographic findings. No EEG-based diagnosis or localization can be made.
