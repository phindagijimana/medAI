Clinical Indication:
Routine EEG in a patient with drug-resistant focal epilepsy characterized by focal impaired-awareness seizures, with onset at age 13 years and a frequency of approximately three seizures per year. Etiology is unknown. Current antiseizure medications are oxcarbazepine and perampanel.

Technique:
Routine scalp EEG recording.

Findings:
No EEG tracing or electrographic findings were provided for interpretation. Background activity, interictal abnormalities, and electrographic seizures therefore cannot be assessed.

Impression:
Technically indeterminate study because electrographic data were not available for review. No EEG-based diagnostic or localizing conclusion can be made.
