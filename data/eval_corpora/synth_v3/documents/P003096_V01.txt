Assessment / Impression

This patient has drug-resistant focal epilepsy with focal to bilateral tonic-clonic seizures. Seizures began at age 10 and currently occur approximately four times per week. The continued high seizure burden while taking three antiseizure medications warrants comprehensive epilepsy evaluation. At present, there is insufficient EEG, imaging, and semiologic information to determine seizure onset localization or candidacy for a specific surgical or device-based therapy.
