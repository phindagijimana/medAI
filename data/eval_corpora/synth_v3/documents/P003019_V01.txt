Assessment / Impression

This is an adult patient with focal epilepsy manifesting as focal impaired-awareness seizures, with seizure onset at age 39. The current frequency is approximately three seizures per year. The reported etiology is immune mediated, specifically associated with GAD65 antibody.

The epilepsy is characterized in the referral information as drug responsive, and the patient remains on levetiracetam. Based on the information available, I would not classify the patient as having drug-resistant epilepsy. At the same time, the continuing reported events warrant confirmation of medication dosing, adherence, event characterization, and the basis for the immune attribution before recommending a treatment change.

The present documentation is insufficient to independently determine seizure localization, assess prior EEG or MRI findings, or evaluate the details of the GAD65 workup. Dr. xxx and I discussed the case, including the importance of obtaining the primary diagnostic records rather than relying exclusively on the summary diagnosis.

Recommendations / Plan

1. Continue Keppra (levetiracetam) as currently prescribed pending formal medication reconciliation. The patient should not independently stop or reduce levetiracetam because abrupt changes may increase seizure risk.

2. Confirm the exact levetiracetam dose, frequency, adherence, tolerability, and refill history. Once these details are available, the regimen can be reviewed in the context of the reported frequency of approximately three focal impaired-awareness seizures per year.
