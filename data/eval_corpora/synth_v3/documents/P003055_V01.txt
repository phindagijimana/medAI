Neurology Progress Note

Chief Complaint: Follow-up generalized epilepsy

History of Present Illness:

The patient is a 54-year-old woman with structurally mediated generalized epilepsy characterized by generalized tonic-clonic seizures. Seizure onset was at age 37. As previously documented, brain MRI demonstrated focal cortical dysplasia, which is considered the likely structural etiology. She remains on Tegretol (carbamazepine) monotherapy. Her epilepsy has been responsive to antiseizure medication, although she has not been completely seizure-free.

She reports an average seizure frequency of approximately three generalized tonic-clonic seizures per year. This frequency is consistent with her seizure calendar; exact event dates were not available for review today. The available interval history does not indicate a change in antiseizure medication. No additional antiseizure medications are currently listed. Medication reconciliation is incomplete regarding the exact carbamazepine strength and dosing frequency, and she was asked to confirm these details with the office after checking her prescription bottle or pharmacy record.

Today’s discussion focused on continued seizure prevention, medication monitoring, and safety. Given the ongoing but relatively infrequent convulsive seizures, we reviewed the importance of adherence, maintaining a seizure diary, regular sleep, and avoidance of missed doses. We also discussed that recurrent generalized tonic-clonic seizures continue to carry risks of injury and sudden unexpected death in epilepsy, even when the overall disorder is medication responsive.
