MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 79, reported frequency approximately five per week. Etiology unknown. Drug-responsive on zonisamide. Evaluate for structural epileptogenic lesion.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin-section coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Mild generalized prominence of the ventricles and sulci, commensurate with age-related cerebral volume loss. No hydrocephalus, midline shift, or extra-axial fluid collection.

Brain Parenchyma:

Supratentorial: No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or focal parenchymal lesion. No restricted diffusion or abnormal susceptibility artifact.

Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality within the cerebellum or brainstem.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal volume loss or T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. The amygdalae and parahippocampal gyri are symmetric. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Normal gyral pattern and cortical thickness. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle signal abnormality, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: Scattered small foci of T2/FLAIR hyperintensity are present in the periventricular and subcortical white matter, nonspecific but most compatible with mild chronic small vessel ischemic change.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Symmetric and normal in signal.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal morphology and signal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are maintained.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild mucosal thickening in the ethmoid air cells. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: No significant abnormality on nondedicated imaging.

Impression:

1. No structural epileptogenic lesion identified. Normal and symmetric mesial temporal structures without evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.
2. Mild chronic small vessel ischemic white matter change and age-related cerebral volume loss.
3. No acute intracranial abnormality, mass effect, or hemorrhage.
