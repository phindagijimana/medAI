Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Drug-resistant focal epilepsy with focal to bilateral tonic-clonic seizures, onset at age 70. Currently seizure-free on levetiracetam and topiramate. Evaluation for structural epileptogenic substrate.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Mild generalized cerebral volume loss with proportional prominence of the ventricles and sulci. No hydrocephalus or extra-axial fluid collection.

Brain Parenchyma:  
Supratentorial: Focal encephalomalacia and surrounding T2/FLAIR hyperintense gliosis involving the left lateral frontal lobe, including the middle and inferior frontal gyri. Mild associated ex vacuo prominence of the adjacent left frontal horn. No restricted diffusion, acute intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or midline shift.  
Infratentorial: No focal parenchymal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The hippocampi are symmetric in volume and signal, with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal T2/FLAIR hyperintensity or temporal horn dilatation. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Aside from the chronic left frontal encephalomalacic change, the cortical ribbon is preserved. No gray matter heterotopia, focal cortical dysplasia, polymicrogyria, or other malformation of cortical development is identified.

White Matter: Mild scattered periventricular and subcortical T2/FLAIR hyperintense foci are nonspecific but most compatible with chronic microvascular ischemic change.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal in signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal signal and morphology. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature: Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild mucosal thickening in the ethmoid air cells. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: No acute abnormality within the included orbits.

Impression:

1. Chronic left frontal encephalomalacia with surrounding gliosis, providing a potential structural substrate for focal epilepsy.  
2. No mesial temporal sclerosis or malformation of cortical development.  
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
