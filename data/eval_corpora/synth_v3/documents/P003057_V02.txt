MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 16, occurring approximately monthly and responsive to oxcarbazepine and lamotrigine. Evaluation for structural seizure focus.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin-section coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles are normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, extra-axial collection, or midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma:

Supratentorial: No acute infarct, intracranial hemorrhage, mass, or mass effect. No abnormal restricted diffusion or pathologic susceptibility artifact.

Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality within the brainstem or cerebellum.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The left hippocampus is subjectively decreased in volume compared with the right, with left-right volume asymmetry of approximately 20%. There is associated increased T2/FLAIR signal and loss of normal internal hippocampal architecture. Mild prominence of the left temporal horn is present on an ex vacuo basis. The right hippocampus is normal in volume and signal with preserved internal architecture. Findings are consistent with left hippocampal sclerosis.

Cortex: No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, abnormal gyration, or transmantle signal abnormality to suggest focal cortical dysplasia. No heterotopia or other malformation of cortical development is identified.

White Matter: No significant white matter signal abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature: Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are maintained on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Left mesial temporal sclerosis, characterized by decreased left hippocampal volume, increased T2/FLAIR signal, and loss of internal architecture. This provides a potential structural substrate for focal epilepsy.
2. No additional epileptogenic lesion or malformation of cortical development identified.
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
