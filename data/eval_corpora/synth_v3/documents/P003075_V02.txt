MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Focal epilepsy with focal aware seizures beginning at age 64, approximately two seizures per year; unknown etiology. Drug responsive on brivaracetam and oxcarbazepine. Evaluate for structural seizure focus.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner using an epilepsy protocol. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin-section coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. No intravenous contrast was administered.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles are normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, extra-axial collection, or midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or focal parenchymal lesion. No abnormal restricted diffusion or pathologic susceptibility artifact.

Supratentorial: Scattered small foci of T2/FLAIR hyperintensity are present within the bilateral periventricular and subcortical cerebral white matter. These are nonspecific in distribution and without associated mass effect or restricted diffusion.

Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality within the brainstem or cerebellum.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal volume loss or abnormal T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. The amygdalae and parahippocampal gyri are unremarkable. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Normal gyral and sulcal pattern. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development identified.

White Matter: Mild scattered nonspecific supratentorial white matter T2/FLAIR hyperintensities, most commonly related to chronic microvascular ischemic change in this age group. No callosal lesion.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal in signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal cerebellar volume and foliation. No focal lesion.

Vasculature: Major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: No significant inflammatory disease. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: No gross orbital abnormality.

Impression:

1. Mild scattered nonspecific supratentorial white matter T2/FLAIR hyperintensities, most commonly reflecting chronic microvascular ischemic change.
2. No identified structural epileptogenic lesion. Normal appearance of the bilateral hippocampi without evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis or malformation of cortical development.
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
