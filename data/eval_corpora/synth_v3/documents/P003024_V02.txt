MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date:  xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider:  Dr. xxx  

Indication:  Unknown-onset tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 33, approximately four per month and responsive to topiramate; evaluate for structural epileptogenic lesion.

Comparison:  None

Technique:  Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the temporal lobes, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. No intravenous contrast was administered.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces:  Ventricles are normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus or extra-axial fluid collection. Basal cisterns are patent.

Brain Parenchyma:

Supratentorial:  A 9 mm well-circumscribed lesion is present in the subcortical white matter of the left posterior frontal lobe. The lesion demonstrates heterogeneous internal T1 and T2 signal with a complete peripheral hemosiderin rim and prominent blooming on SWI, compatible with a cavernous malformation. No surrounding edema or evidence of recent hemorrhage. No associated mass effect.

Infratentorial:  No focal parenchymal signal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures:  The hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal volume loss or abnormal T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex:  Gyral pattern and cortical thickness are within normal limits. No focal cortical dysplasia, gray matter heterotopia, polymicrogyria, or other malformation of cortical development is identified.

White Matter:  No additional focal white matter signal abnormality. No restricted diffusion to indicate acute infarction.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami:  Normal in signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum:  Normal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences):  Major intracranial arterial flow voids are preserved. No additional vascular malformation is evident on this nonangiographic examination.

Skull Base and Calvarium:  No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells:  Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits:  Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Subcentimeter left posterior frontal cavernous malformation with chronic hemosiderin deposition. No surrounding edema, mass effect, or evidence of recent hemorrhage. This represents a potential structural epileptogenic focus.
2. Normal appearance of the bilateral hippocampi without mesial temporal sclerosis. No malformation of cortical development.
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
