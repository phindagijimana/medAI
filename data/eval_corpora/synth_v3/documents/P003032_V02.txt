Technique:  Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner. No intravenous contrast was administered.

Supratentorial: Gray-white matter differentiation is preserved. No focal encephalomalacia or gliosis. No MRI evidence of prior cortical infarction or traumatic injury.

Infratentorial: No focal abnormality within the brainstem or cerebellum.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture and normal morphology of the hippocampal formations. No hippocampal volume loss or T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. The amygdalae and parahippocampal gyri are symmetric. No mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: No cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, abnormal gyration, transmantle sign, or other evidence of focal cortical dysplasia. No heterotopia or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: No significant white matter signal abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Symmetric and normal in signal.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal morphology and signal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature: Expected intracranial arterial flow voids are preserved on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered paranasal sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable within the limits of this examination.
