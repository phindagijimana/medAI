Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality or mass effect.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The bilateral hippocampi are symmetric in size and T2/FLAIR signal, with preserved internal architecture. The hippocampal formations and parahippocampal gyri are morphologically normal. No mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Normal cortical thickness and gyral pattern. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white matter junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, polymicrogyria, schizencephaly, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: Normal white matter volume and signal. No focal gliosis or encephalomalacia.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Symmetric and normal in signal.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal morphology and signal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid air cell mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Normal noncontrast MRI of the brain performed with epilepsy protocol. No structural epileptogenic substrate identified.
