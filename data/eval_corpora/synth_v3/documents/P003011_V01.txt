Assessment / Impression

This patient has combined generalized and focal epilepsy, with generalized tonic-clonic seizures documented as the presenting seizure type. Seizures began at age 29 and have been reported at a frequency of approximately five per month. The etiology is categorized as metabolic.

The condition is designated as drug-responsive, and the patient is currently treated with topiramate. However, the absence of the current dose, dosing schedule, treatment timeline, and seizure-frequency trend limits assessment of the degree of response. Drug-responsive epilepsy does not necessarily imply seizure freedom, particularly given the separately reported frequency of five seizures per month. These points should be reconciled with the patient and referring records.

The available information is not sufficient to determine whether the generalized tonic-clonic seizures have generalized onset, focal-to-bilateral evolution, or both at different times. That distinction is particularly relevant in a patient whose overall epilepsy type has been classified as combined generalized and focal. Prior EEG and MRI data will be important for confirming the working classification. No conclusion regarding candidacy for VNS, RNS, DBS, resective surgery, or other procedural treatment can be made from the limited records currently available.
