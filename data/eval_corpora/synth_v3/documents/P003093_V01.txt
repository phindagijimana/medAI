Assessment / Impression

This patient has focal structural epilepsy beginning at age 69, manifested by focal to bilateral tonic-clonic seizures. The identified structural abnormality is encephalomalacia on MRI. The epilepsy has been categorized as drug-resistant, and the patient is maintained on a three-drug regimen consisting of topiramate, carbamazepine, and perampanel.

Encouragingly, the current reported seizure frequency is zero per month. Therefore, there is no immediate indication from the information available today to disrupt a regimen that is associated with current seizure freedom. At the same time, the number of ASMs and the prior designation of drug resistance warrant careful longitudinal follow-up and confirmation of the treatment history.

A complete assessment of candidacy for epilepsy surgery or neuromodulation would require substantially more localization data than are currently available. This would generally include direct review of MRI images and EEG data, as well as detailed documentation of the habitual seizure semiology and prior medication trials. Given the present seizure-free status, any further presurgical evaluation should be individualized rather than pursued solely on the basis of the structural MRI finding.
