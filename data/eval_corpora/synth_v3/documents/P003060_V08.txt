Indication: Focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 38, occurring approximately four times per month; unknown etiology, drug responsive on lamotrigine and valproate. Evaluate for structural epileptogenic substrate.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the temporal lobes, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, double inversion recovery, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. No intravenous contrast was administered.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles and sulci are normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus or extra-axial fluid collection. No midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or focal parenchymal signal abnormality. No restricted diffusion or abnormal susceptibility artifact.

Supratentorial: Normal cerebral volume and signal. No focal encephalomalacia or gliosis.

Infratentorial: Normal signal and morphology of the brainstem and cerebellum.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal T2/FLAIR hyperintensity or volume loss. The fornices and mammillary bodies are symmetric. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Normal cortical thickness and gyral pattern. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle signal abnormality, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: Normal for age. No focal or confluent T2/FLAIR hyperintense white matter lesion.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Symmetric and normal in signal.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: No focal abnormality. Cerebellar volume is preserved.

Vasculature: Expected flow voids are maintained within the major intracranial vessels.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion. The craniocervical junction is unremarkable.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild mucosal thickening in the ethmoid air cells. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Normal MRI of the brain performed with epilepsy protocol. No structural epileptogenic substrate identified.
2. Symmetric normal-appearing hippocampi without evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.
