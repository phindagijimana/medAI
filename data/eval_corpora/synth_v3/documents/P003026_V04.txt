Recommendations:  
1. Discuss the documented epilepsy history at PRC, while recognizing that neuropsychological conclusions remain pending.  
2. Complete a comprehensive epilepsy-focused neuropsychological evaluation, including formal measures of performance validity.  
3. Obtain detailed developmental, educational, occupational, psychiatric, and functional histories, as well as a complete antiseizure medication and treatment history.  
4. Integrate neuropsychological results with video-EEG and structural and functional imaging before determining cognitive lateralization or surgical risk.  
5. If a dominant-hemisphere intervention is under consideration, obtain language mapping with task-based fMRI. Consider Wada testing if language dominance remains uncertain or if findings are discordant.  
6. Establish a valid preintervention cognitive baseline and repeat neuropsychological assessment approximately 6–12 months after surgery or another major treatment intervention.
