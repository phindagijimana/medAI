Cortex: No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, abnormal gyral pattern, transmantle signal abnormality, or heterotopic gray matter. No evidence of focal cortical dysplasia or other malformation of cortical development.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.
