Indication: Drug-resistant focal epilepsy of unknown etiology, onset at age 5, with focal-to-bilateral tonic-clonic seizures occurring approximately three times per week despite current valproate and topiramate therapy. Evaluate for structural epileptogenic lesion.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin-section coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. No intravenous contrast was administered.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles are normal in size and configuration. Basal cisterns are patent. No hydrocephalus, extra-axial collection, or midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma:

Supratentorial: No focal parenchymal signal abnormality, mass effect, restricted diffusion, or susceptibility artifact. No evidence of prior intracranial hemorrhage.

Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality involving the brainstem or cerebellum.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture and normal morphology. No hippocampal T2/FLAIR hyperintensity or volume loss. The amygdalae and parahippocampal gyri are symmetric. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Cortical thickness and gyral pattern are within normal limits. Gray-white matter differentiation is preserved. No focal cortical dysplasia, heterotopia, polymicrogyria, schizencephaly, or other malformation of cortical development is identified.

White Matter: Normal signal and volume for age. No focal gliosis or encephalomalacia. Corpus callosum is normally formed.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal and symmetric in signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal morphology and signal. Cerebellar tonsils are normally positioned.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Expected major intracranial arterial flow voids are maintained.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion. The sella and craniocervical junction are unremarkable.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Minimal mucosal thickening in the maxillary sinuses. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable on nondedicated imaging.

Impression:

1. No structural epileptogenic lesion identified.  
2. Symmetric, normal-appearing hippocampi without evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis. No malformation of cortical development.  
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
