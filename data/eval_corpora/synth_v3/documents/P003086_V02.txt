MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Drug-resistant generalized epilepsy with typical absence seizures, onset at age 74; structural etiology suspected. Currently seizure-free (0 seizures per month) on valproate, lacosamide, perampanel, and lamotrigine.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Mild generalized prominence of the ventricles and sulci. No hydrocephalus or extra-axial fluid collection. No midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or vasogenic edema. No abnormal susceptibility deposition.

Supratentorial: Scattered punctate and patchy T2/FLAIR hyperintensities within the periventricular and subcortical white matter are nonspecific but most compatible with mild chronic small vessel ischemic change.

Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The left hippocampus is subjectively decreased in volume compared with the right, with left-right asymmetry of approximately 20%. There is associated increased T2/FLAIR signal and loss of normal internal hippocampal architecture. Mild prominence of the left temporal horn and choroidal fissure is present. The right hippocampus is normal in volume and signal with preserved internal architecture.

Cortex: No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, abnormal gyration, or transmantle signal abnormality. No heterotopic gray matter or other malformation of cortical development is identified.

White Matter: Mild chronic microvascular-type signal changes as described above. No focal encephalomalacia.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal in signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: No focal lesion. Cerebellar volume is maintained.

Vasculature: Expected major intracranial arterial flow voids are preserved.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No suspicious marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered paranasal sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: No acute abnormality.

Impression:

1. Left mesial temporal sclerosis, characterized by asymmetric left hippocampal volume loss, increased T2/FLAIR signal, and loss of internal architecture.
2. Mild generalized cerebral volume loss and mild chronic small vessel ischemic white matter change.
3. No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or additional epileptogenic structural lesion identified.
