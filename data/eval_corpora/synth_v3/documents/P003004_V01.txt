Neurology Progress Note

Chief Complaint: Follow-up drug-resistant focal epilepsy

History of Present Illness:  
The patient is a 48-year-old man with focal epilepsy of unknown etiology, with seizure onset at age 22, who returns for routine follow-up. As documented in prior notes, his habitual events are focal aware seizures. His current antiseizure regimen consists of Depakote (valproate), Fycompa (perampanel), and Topamax (topiramate). Despite combination therapy, seizures have remained refractory.

Since the last visit, he continues to average approximately 2 focal aware seizures per month. He tracks events on a seizure calendar and reports no clear sustained improvement in frequency. No antiseizure medication changes have been made since the prior visit. He reports taking his medications consistently. He has not identified a reliable trigger, although sleep deprivation and missed medication were reviewed as potential seizure precipitants. No interval injury or emergency department visit related to a seizure was reported.

His primary concern today is the persistence of breakthrough seizures despite three antiseizure medications. The ongoing events remain disruptive to daily activities and quality of life. Medication adverse effects were reviewed, including sedation, dizziness, cognitive slowing, mood or behavioral change, weight change, and tremor; he does not identify a new or clearly progressive adverse effect today.
