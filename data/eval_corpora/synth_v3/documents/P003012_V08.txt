Medical Decision-Making (MDM): Presentation is most consistent with a breakthrough focal aware seizure in a patient with known focal structural epilepsy. She is afebrile, hemodynamically stable, and back to baseline without focal deficit. No evidence of status epilepticus, CNS infection, significant metabolic trigger, acute intoxication, or traumatic intracranial injury. No benzodiazepine or IV antiseizure loading dose was indicated because the event had terminated and there was no recurrence during observation.

Differential included epileptic seizure, syncope, TIA, and psychogenic nonepileptic event. Syncope is less likely given retained awareness and known stereotyped focal seizure history. TIA is less likely without a focal negative neurologic symptom or residual deficit. After neurology input and ED observation, patient remains neurologically intact and appropriate for outpatient management.

Assessment:  
1. Breakthrough focal aware seizure.  
2. Drug-resistant focal epilepsy secondary to hippocampal sclerosis.

Disposition: Discharge home in stable condition. Continue all home ASMs without missed doses; no ED medication changes. Close follow-up with epilepsy neurology. Reviewed seizure precautions, including no driving unless cleared under applicable state law, no swimming or bathing alone, no climbing, and no operation of dangerous machinery. Return immediately for seizure lasting >5 minutes, repeated seizures without recovery, injury, fever, persistent confusion, new weakness, severe headache, or other worsening symptoms.
