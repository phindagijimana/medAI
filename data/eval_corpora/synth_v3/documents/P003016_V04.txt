Recommendations:  
1. Review the available clinical, MRI, and electrophysiologic findings at PRC, with specific attention to concordance between the generalized epilepsy classification, myoclonic semiology, and hippocampal sclerosis.  
2. Obtain the full MRI report and images to establish whether hippocampal sclerosis is left-sided, right-sided, or bilateral.  
3. Complete or retrieve a comprehensive neuropsychological evaluation, including objective performance-validity testing, before drawing conclusions about cognitive lateralization or surgical risk.  
4. If a focal surgical intervention is under consideration, obtain fMRI language mapping. Consider Wada testing if fMRI is inconclusive or discordant with other localization data.  
5. Review cognitive adverse effects of carbamazepine and topiramate in the context of seizure control. Medication changes should be directed by the treating epileptologist.  
6. If surgery is pursued, repeat neuropsychological testing approximately 6 to 12 months postoperatively to document cognitive outcome and guide rehabilitation.
