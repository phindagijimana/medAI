Cortex: Normal cortical thickness and gyral pattern. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, polymicrogyria, or other malformation of cortical development.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Symmetric and normal in signal.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal morphology and signal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild mucosal thickening in the ethmoid air cells. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. No structural epileptogenic lesion identified.
