MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Generalized epilepsy with myoclonic seizures beginning at age 28, occurring approximately 7 times per week. Suspected structural etiology. Drug-responsive on valproate.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal T2/FLAIR images through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles and sulci are within normal limits for age. No hydrocephalus, midline shift, or extra-axial collection.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or vasogenic edema. No abnormal restricted diffusion or pathologic susceptibility artifact.

Supratentorial: No focal parenchymal lesion. Gray-white matter differentiation is maintained.

Infratentorial: No focal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The left hippocampus is subjectively decreased in volume compared with the right, with left-right volume asymmetry of approximately 20%. There is associated increased T2/FLAIR signal and loss of normal internal hippocampal architecture. Mild enlargement of the left temporal horn and collateral sulcus is present on an ex vacuo basis. The right hippocampus is normal in volume and signal with preserved internal architecture. Findings are compatible with left hippocampal sclerosis.

Cortex: No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, abnormal gyral pattern, transmantle sign, or other evidence of focal cortical dysplasia. No heterotopic gray matter or polymicrogyria.

White Matter: No significant white matter signal abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal in signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal. No cerebellar volume loss or tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Left mesial temporal sclerosis, characterized by decreased left hippocampal volume, increased T2/FLAIR signal, and loss of internal architecture. Correlation with electroclinical localization is recommended.
2. No focal cortical dysplasia or other malformation of cortical development.
3. No acute intracranial hemorrhage, infarction, mass effect, or hydrocephalus.
