This patient has a reported diagnosis of drug-resistant generalized epilepsy with typical absence seizures beginning at age 78. The epilepsy has been attributed to an infectious etiology. The current seizure-free status is reassuring, although the duration of seizure freedom and the exact medication doses are not known.
