Indication:  Unknown-onset tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 68, reportedly occurring approximately five times per month and responsive to zonisamide; etiology unknown. Evaluate for structural epileptogenic substrate.

Technique:  Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. No intravenous contrast was administered.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces:  Ventricles and sulci are within normal limits for age. No hydrocephalus, midline shift, extra-axial collection, or intracranial mass effect.

Brain Parenchyma:

Supratentorial:  No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass, or abnormal susceptibility. No encephalomalacia or focal gliosis is identified.

Infratentorial:  No focal signal abnormality or restricted diffusion.

Mesial Temporal Structures:  The hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal volume loss or T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. The hippocampal formations, amygdalae, and parahippocampal gyri are unremarkable. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex:  Gyral formation and cortical thickness are within normal limits. No focal cortical dysplasia, polymicrogyria, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development is identified. No focal blurring of the gray-white matter junction.

White Matter:  Minimal scattered punctate supratentorial white matter FLAIR hyperintensities are nonspecific and within expected limits for age. No confluent white matter abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami:  Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum:  Normal signal and volume. Cerebellar tonsils are normally positioned.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences):  Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are maintained.

Skull Base and Calvarium:  No suspicious marrow-replacing lesion. The sella and craniocervical junction are unremarkable.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells:  Mild mucosal thickening in the ethmoid air cells. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits:  No significant abnormality.

Impression:

1. No structural epileptogenic lesion identified.  
2. Symmetric, normal-appearing hippocampi without evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis. No malformation of cortical development.  
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
