Brain Parenchyma:

Mesial Temporal Structures: The hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture and normal morphology. No hippocampal volume loss or T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. The amygdalae and parahippocampal gyri are unremarkable. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Normal cortical thickness and gyral pattern. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white matter junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, polymicrogyria, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: No significant white matter disease. The corpus callosum is normally formed.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Symmetric and normal in signal.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal morphology and signal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion. The sella and craniocervical junction are unremarkable.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered paranasal sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: No acute orbital abnormality.

Impression:

1. Normal noncontrast MRI of the brain performed with epilepsy protocol. No structural epileptogenic lesion identified.
2. Symmetric, normal-appearing hippocampi without evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.
