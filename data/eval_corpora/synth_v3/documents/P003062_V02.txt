Indication: Generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 36, reported frequency up to 7 per week; unknown etiology, drug responsive on zonisamide. Evaluate for structural seizure focus.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles are normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, extra-axial fluid collection, or midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or focal parenchymal signal abnormality. No restricted diffusion or abnormal susceptibility artifact.

Supratentorial: Normal cerebral volume and signal for age. No focal encephalomalacia or gliosis.

Infratentorial: No focal abnormality within the brainstem or cerebellum.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The hippocampi are symmetric in size and T2/FLAIR signal, with preserved internal architecture and normal morphology. No hippocampal volume loss or temporal horn dilatation. The amygdalae and parahippocampal gyri are symmetric. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Gyral formation and cortical thickness are within normal limits. No focal cortical dysplasia, heterotopia, polymicrogyria, or other malformation of cortical development is identified.

White Matter: No significant white matter disease. Corpus callosum is normally formed.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Symmetric and normal in signal.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal morphology and signal. Cerebellar tonsils are normally positioned.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No suspicious marrow-replacing lesion. A 7 mm simple-appearing pineal cyst is present without mass effect or hydrocephalus.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Minimal mucosal thickening within the maxillary sinuses. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable on nondedicated imaging.

Impression:

1. No structural epileptogenic lesion identified. No mesial temporal sclerosis or malformation of cortical development.  
2. Incidental 7 mm simple-appearing pineal cyst without mass effect or hydrocephalus.  
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
