Indication: Combined generalized and focal epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures, onset at age 29 and reported frequency of approximately 5 seizures per month. Reported metabolic etiology and drug-responsive status; currently treated with topiramate. Evaluate for structural epileptogenic lesion.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin-section coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. No intravenous contrast was administered.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles are normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, midline shift, extra-axial fluid collection, or abnormal mass effect.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass, or edema. No abnormal restricted diffusion or pathologic susceptibility artifact.

Supratentorial: Cerebral hemispheres demonstrate normal volume and signal for age. No focal encephalomalacia or gliosis.

Infratentorial: No focal abnormality of the brainstem or cerebellum.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Bilateral hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture and normal morphology. No hippocampal volume loss or T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. The amygdalae and parahippocampal gyri are unremarkable. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Normal gyral and sulcal pattern. No cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle signal abnormality, heterotopia, polymicrogyria, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: No focal or confluent white matter signal abnormality. Corpus callosum is normally formed.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Symmetric and normal in signal.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal morphology and signal. Cerebellar tonsils are normally positioned.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion. Pituitary gland is within normal limits for size.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Normal MRI appearance of the brain. No structural epileptogenic lesion identified.
2. Symmetric normal-appearing hippocampi without evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis or malformation of cortical development.
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
