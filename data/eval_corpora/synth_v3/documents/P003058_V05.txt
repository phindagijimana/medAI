His principal ongoing concern is continued breakthrough absence seizures despite four concurrent antiseizure medications. We discussed that this seizure burden remains clinically meaningful and is consistent with drug-resistant epilepsy. Any medication adjustment should be gradual and undertaken only after confirming his exact doses, adherence, prior medication trials, and available EEG data. He was encouraged to continue maintaining a seizure diary, including dates, duration, possible triggers, missed doses, and any change in seizure semiology.
