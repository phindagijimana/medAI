No Ativan (lorazepam) or additional IV antiseizure load was indicated because seizure activity had terminated prior to arrival and there was no recurrence. Patient tolerated PO and ambulated independently. Neurology agrees with outpatient management. Strict return precautions reviewed.

Assessment:

Disposition: Discharged home in stable condition with responsible adult. Continue valproate and topiramate without missed doses. Follow up with epilepsy/neurology clinic within 1–2 weeks. Seizure precautions reviewed: no driving per applicable state law, swimming alone, bathing unattended, climbing heights, or operating heavy machinery.
