ASM Wean:

- Day 0: Continue the verified home regimen while baseline EEG is obtained.  
- If no habitual event or sufficient epileptiform activity is recorded, reduce brivaracetam to approximately 50% of the verified home total daily dose on Day 1, then hold beginning Day 2 if clinically safe.
