Assessment / Impression

The patient has focal epilepsy manifested by focal aware seizures, with onset at age 26. The current reported frequency is approximately one seizure per month. Seizures have continued during treatment with lacosamide, oxcarbazepine, and perampanel. This clinical course is consistent with drug-resistant focal epilepsy.

The etiology is currently unknown. Localization also cannot be determined from the information available because detailed semiology, EEG data, and neuroimaging were not provided. Before recommending a specific medication adjustment or procedural therapy, we need to verify the current doses, treatment adherence, tolerability, and prior ASM trials. We also need to confirm that the habitual episodes are epileptic seizures and determine whether there is a reproducible electroclinical onset.

Given continued seizures on three ASMs, a comprehensive presurgical epilepsy evaluation is reasonable. This does not imply that resective surgery will necessarily be recommended. Depending on the results of VEEG and imaging, potential options may eventually include resection or ablation, VNS, RNS, DBS, or continued medical management.
