MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Generalized epilepsy with myoclonic seizures beginning at age 28, currently approximately twice monthly and drug responsive on oxcarbazepine. Structural etiology; evaluate known hippocampal sclerosis.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles are normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, midline shift, extra-axial collection, or abnormal mass effect.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, or mass lesion. No restricted diffusion or abnormal susceptibility artifact.

Supratentorial: No focal encephalomalacia or additional epileptogenic lesion is identified.

Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The left hippocampal volume is subjectively decreased compared with the right, with left-right asymmetry of approximately 20%. There is associated T2/FLAIR hyperintensity and loss of normal internal hippocampal architecture. Mild prominence of the left temporal horn is present on an ex vacuo basis. The right hippocampus is normal in volume and signal with preserved internal architecture. The amygdalae are unremarkable.

Cortex: Normal gyral and sulcal pattern. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: No significant white matter signal abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are maintained.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Findings consistent with left mesial temporal sclerosis, including decreased left hippocampal volume, T2/FLAIR hyperintensity, and loss of internal architecture.
2. No additional structural epileptogenic lesion or malformation of cortical development identified. Correlation with electroclinical localization is recommended given the reported generalized epilepsy phenotype.
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
