MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Focal epilepsy of unknown etiology with focal to bilateral tonic-clonic seizures, onset at age 24 and reported frequency of approximately three per month; drug responsive on oxcarbazepine and zonisamide. Evaluate for structural seizure focus.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin-section coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles are normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, midline shift, or extra-axial fluid collection.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or abnormal susceptibility. No focal encephalomalacia or gliosis.

Supratentorial: No focal parenchymal signal abnormality or structural lesion identified to suggest a seizure focus. An incidental 8 mm pineal cyst is present without tectal mass effect or aqueductal obstruction.

Infratentorial: No focal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal T2/FLAIR hyperintensity or volume loss. The amygdalae and parahippocampal gyri are symmetric. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Gyral formation and cortical thickness are within normal limits. No gray matter heterotopia, focal cortical dysplasia, polymicrogyria, or other malformation of cortical development is identified.

White Matter: Normal for age. No pathologic T2/FLAIR hyperintense white matter lesion.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal signal and volume. Cerebellar tonsils are normally positioned.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Minimal mucosal thickening in the maxillary sinuses. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: No significant abnormality.

Impression:

1. No structural seizure focus identified. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis or malformation of cortical development.
2. Incidental 8 mm pineal cyst without mass effect or hydrocephalus.
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
