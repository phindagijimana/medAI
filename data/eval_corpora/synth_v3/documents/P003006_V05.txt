Neurology Progress Note

Chief Complaint: Follow-up focal epilepsy

History of Present Illness:  
This is a 15-year-old right-handed male with focal epilepsy of presumed infectious etiology, with onset at age 10, presenting for routine follow-up. His habitual seizures are focal impaired-awareness seizures characterized by behavioral arrest and decreased responsiveness, as documented in prior notes. He is currently treated with Fycompa (perampanel) 6 mg nightly. His epilepsy has been drug-responsive, although he continues to have intermittent breakthrough events.

He was last seen on 06/21/2026. Since that visit, he and his parent report no significant change in seizure pattern. Review of the seizure calendar indicates an average of approximately two focal impaired-awareness seizures per month. Events remain brief and self-limited, without reported progression to bilateral tonic-clonic activity. There have been no prolonged seizures, clusters requiring rescue medication, injuries, emergency department visits, or hospitalizations. No antiseizure medication changes have been made since the prior visit, and adherence is reported as good without routinely missed doses.

He is tolerating perampanel without excessive daytime sedation, dizziness, gait difficulty, or concerning mood/behavioral changes. He denies new headaches, weakness, sensory symptoms, vision changes, or sleep disturbance. He remains active in school and reports no major change in academic performance or daily functioning. The family’s primary concern today is whether the residual seizure frequency warrants a medication adjustment. Seizure precautions and the importance of maintaining an accurate diary were reviewed.
