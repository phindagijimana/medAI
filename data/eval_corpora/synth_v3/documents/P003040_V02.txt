Indication:  Drug-resistant epilepsy of unknown type with unknown-onset tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 54; structural etiology suspected. Seizure-free over the past month on brivaracetam and carbamazepine. Evaluate for epileptogenic substrate.

Comparison:  None.

Technique:  Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin-section coronal imaging through the temporal lobes, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles and sulci are within normal limits for age. No hydrocephalus, midline shift, or extra-axial collection.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or vasogenic edema. No abnormal restricted diffusion or pathologic susceptibility artifact.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Hippocampal volume loss is present with associated T2/FLAIR hyperintensity and loss of normal internal hippocampal architecture, compatible with hippocampal sclerosis. No mesial temporal mass or diffusion restriction is identified.

Cortex: Cortical morphology and gyral pattern are preserved. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, polymicrogyria, or other malformation of cortical development is identified.

White Matter: A few punctate supratentorial white matter FLAIR hyperintensities are nonspecific and within expected limits for age. No confluent white matter disease.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal signal and volume. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature: Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No suspicious marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered paranasal sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: No significant abnormality on nondedicated imaging.

Impression:

1. Hippocampal volume loss, T2/FLAIR hyperintensity, and loss of internal architecture, consistent with hippocampal sclerosis and a potential structural epileptogenic substrate.
2. No focal cortical dysplasia or other malformation of cortical development identified.
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
