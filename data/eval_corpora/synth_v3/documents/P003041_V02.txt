   - Continue Depakote (valproate) and Keppra (levetiracetam) pending complete medication reconciliation. The family should confirm tablet or liquid formulation, strength, dose, and administration times before any adjustment is made.

   - Given ongoing frequent seizures despite two current medications, proceed with comprehensive drug-resistant epilepsy evaluation, including prolonged video-EEG monitoring and epilepsy-protocol brain MRI if not already completed.

   - Reviewed seizure first aid and safety precautions, including supervision around water, avoidance of heights and open flames, showering rather than bathing when feasible, and use of appropriate protective equipment.
   - Family instructed to call 911 for a convulsive seizure lasting 5 minutes or longer, repeated seizures without return to baseline, significant injury, respiratory compromise, or an event substantially different from his typical seizures.
