Medical Decision-Making (MDM):  
Presentation is most consistent with focal-onset convulsive status epilepticus in a patient with known structural, drug-resistant focal epilepsy. Differential included seizure due to subtherapeutic medication level, metabolic derangement, infection, toxic ingestion, acute intracranial process, syncope, TIA, and psychogenic non-epileptic event. Prolonged witnessed rhythmic activity, focal onset, progression to bilateral convulsions, and absent recovery strongly support epileptic status. No fever or meningismus to suggest CNS infection, and CT shows no acute structural emergency.

Patient required benzodiazepine, second-line IV antiseizure loading, airway control, and anesthetic infusion. Hemodynamics remained stable post-intubation. Critical care provided for active status epilepticus, respiratory failure/airway compromise, repeated reassessment, consultant discussion, and disposition coordination.

Assessment:  
1. Focal-onset convulsive status epilepticus.  
2. Drug-resistant focal epilepsy secondary to focal cortical dysplasia.  
3. Acute respiratory failure requiring endotracheal intubation.

Disposition: Admit to neurocritical care under Neurology for continuous EEG and ongoing seizure management. Seizure precautions instituted. Family updated regarding critical condition and plan.
