Indication:  Generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 57, occurring approximately once monthly; unknown etiology, drug responsive on carbamazepine. Evaluate for structural seizure substrate.

Comparison:  None

Technique:  Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the temporal lobes, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. No intravenous contrast was administered.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles are normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, midline shift, extra-axial collection, or abnormal extra-axial mass.

Brain Parenchyma:

Supratentorial: Brain parenchymal volume is within normal limits for age. No focal parenchymal signal abnormality, mass effect, acute hemorrhage, or restricted diffusion. No encephalomalacia or chronic cortical infarct.

Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality or mass within the brainstem or cerebellum.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture and normal morphology. No hippocampal volume loss or T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. The amygdalae and parahippocampal gyri are symmetric. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Cortical thickness and gyral pattern are within normal limits. No focal cortical dysplasia, heterotopia, polymicrogyria, or other malformation of cortical development is identified.

White Matter: No significant white matter signal abnormality. The corpus callosum is normally formed.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal and symmetric in signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal cerebellar volume and folial pattern. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved. No abnormal susceptibility artifact to suggest prior hemorrhage or cavernous malformation.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion. The sella and craniocervical junction are unremarkable.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Minimal mucosal thickening in the ethmoid air cells. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Normal MRI appearance of the brain. No structural epileptogenic lesion identified.
2. Symmetric normal hippocampi without evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
