Assessment / Impression

This is an adult patient with seizures beginning at age 33 and characterized, on the currently available information, as unknown-onset tonic-clonic seizures. The reported frequency is approximately four per month. The epilepsy type remains unknown because there is not yet sufficient clinical or EEG information to determine whether the seizures are focal to bilateral tonic-clonic or generalized tonic-clonic from onset.

The reported vascular malformation raises concern for a structural cause of epilepsy. Determining whether the lesion is causally related to the seizures will require review of the actual MRI images, detailed seizure semiology, and electrophysiologic data. At present, the most appropriate etiologic classification is structural epilepsy, with electroclinical classification pending.

Before considering a medication adjustment, we need to verify the current topiramate dose and frequency, adherence, treatment response, and any prior antiseizure medication exposure.
