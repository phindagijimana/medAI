MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Drug-resistant focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 33, currently occurring approximately twice yearly despite topiramate and brivaracetam. Known structural etiology/focal cortical dysplasia.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner using an epilepsy protocol. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin-section imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. No intravenous contrast was administered.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles are normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, extra-axial collection, or midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma:

Supratentorial: Focal cortical thickening with blurring of the gray-white matter junction and mild subjacent subcortical T2/FLAIR hyperintensity is present at the known cortical seizure focus. The imaging appearance is compatible with focal cortical dysplasia. No associated mass effect, restricted diffusion, hemorrhage, or susceptibility abnormality. No additional focal parenchymal lesion is identified.

Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality or restricted diffusion.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal volume loss or abnormal T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. The amygdalae and parahippocampal gyri are unremarkable. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Aside from the focal cortical dysplasia described above, cortical gyration and sulcation are within normal limits. No additional cortical thickening, gray-white junction blurring, heterotopia, polymicrogyria, or schizencephaly.

White Matter: No additional significant white matter signal abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal in signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal. No cerebellar volume loss.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Major intracranial arterial flow voids are preserved.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered paranasal sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Focal cortical dysplasia at the known cortical seizure focus, with cortical thickening, gray-white junction blurring, and mild subjacent T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. Findings are consistent with the reported Palmini type IIa focal cortical dysplasia.
2. No mesial temporal sclerosis or additional malformation of cortical development.
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
