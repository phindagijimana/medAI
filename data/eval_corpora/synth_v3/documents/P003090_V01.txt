Assessment / Impression

The patient has focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 24. The current reported seizure burden is approximately five events per month. The epilepsy is attributed to a genetic etiology on the basis of a causative SCN1A c.5734C>T variant.

The available records designate the epilepsy as drug responsive. The current ASM regimen consists of levetiracetam and oxcarbazepine, although doses and frequencies were not provided. Given the ongoing reported seizure frequency, medication reconciliation and review of the longitudinal response to therapy are needed to understand the degree of seizure control and to guide any future adjustment.

The MRI finding of nonspecific white matter changes does not provide additional localization based on the information available. Prior EEG data were not available, so electrographic classification and localization cannot be further addressed from the submitted records.

At this time, the supplied information does not establish drug-resistant epilepsy. A presurgical workup with EMU admission, VNS, RNS, DBS, or resective surgery evaluation is therefore not being initiated on the basis of the current documentation alone. This can be reconsidered if subsequent records demonstrate persistent disabling seizures despite adequately selected and tolerated ASM trials.
