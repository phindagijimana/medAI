She meets criteria for drug-resistant epilepsy. Her current antiseizure medication regimen consists of Topamax (topiramate) and Briviact (brivaracetam). A complete history of previously tried ASMs, maximum tolerated doses, treatment response, and adverse effects is not available in the current source material. Despite a relatively low current seizure frequency of approximately two events per year, continued seizures on her current regimen and the presence of a structural lesion support further presurgical evaluation. The patient is being admitted to the dedicated EMU for continuous EEG with synchronized video, supervised medication adjustment, activation procedures, and seizure precautions. The anticipated duration of monitoring is five days, although the final duration will depend on EEG findings, medication tolerance, and whether a habitual event is captured.
