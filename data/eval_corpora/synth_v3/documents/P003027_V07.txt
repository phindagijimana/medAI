Indication: Presurgical characterization of drug-resistant focal epilepsy with focal aware seizures. Seizure onset occurred at age 66, with a current frequency of approximately two seizures per year despite treatment with lamotrigine, levetiracetam, and oxcarbazepine. The suspected etiology is structural; prior MRI demonstrates hippocampal sclerosis.

AED Changes During Admission: Home lamotrigine, levetiracetam, and oxcarbazepine were reduced and then held sequentially to facilitate event capture. Medications were restarted before discharge.

Technique: Continuous video EEG monitoring was performed using the international 10-20 system with additional anterior temporal electrodes. Digital EEG and synchronized video were reviewed continuously. Activation procedures included photic stimulation and hyperventilation when clinically appropriate.

Background: The awake background was organized and symmetric, consisting of a 9 Hz posterior dominant rhythm reactive to eye opening and closure, with an appropriate anterior-to-posterior frequency and amplitude gradient. Low-amplitude beta activity was present anteriorly. Drowsiness was characterized by attenuation of the PDR and emergence of diffuse theta activity. Stage N2 sleep contained symmetric vertex waves, sleep spindles, and K complexes. Slow-wave and REM sleep were recorded. No persistent generalized background abnormality was present.

Interictal Findings:

1. Frequent left anterior temporal sharp waves, maximal at F7/FT9 with a field involving T7, becoming more abundant during drowsiness and sleep.
2. Occasional brief runs of left temporal intermittent rhythmic delta activity (TIRDA), maximal over F7-T7, lasting 3–8 seconds without clinical correlate.
3. Intermittent polymorphic 2–4 Hz delta slowing over the left temporal region.
4. No independent right hemispheric IEDs, generalized spike-wave discharges, LPDs, or GPDs were identified.

Ictal Events:

Total events captured: 2 electroclinical seizures.

Event 1 (Day 3, 14:26):  
Behavioral: The patient reported a stereotyped internal rising sensation and remained responsive, followed commands, and recalled the event. No definite automatisms or convulsive activity occurred. Clinical duration was approximately 50 seconds.  
EEG: Initial rhythmic 5–6 Hz theta activity appeared over the left anterior temporal region, maximal at F7/FT9, evolving in amplitude and slowing to 3–4 Hz delta with limited spread across the left temporal chain.  
Post-ictal: Transient left temporal delta slowing persisted for approximately 2 minutes.

Event 2 (Day 4, 09:11):  
Behavioral: The patient activated the event button and described a sensation typical of habitual seizures. Awareness and language were preserved throughout. Duration was approximately 45 seconds.  
EEG: Rhythmic sharply contoured theta arose over F7/FT9 and T7, demonstrating evolution in frequency and morphology before abrupt termination. No contralateral propagation was evident.  
Post-ictal: Mild left temporal slowing was present for approximately 90 seconds.

Impression:

1. Abnormal long-term video EEG due to frequent left anterior temporal IEDs, left TIRDA, and intermittent left temporal slowing.
2. Two habitual focal aware seizures were recorded, both demonstrating left anterior temporal electrographic onset.
3. These findings support a diagnosis of focal epilepsy localized to the left temporal region. In the clinical context of MRI-demonstrated hippocampal sclerosis and persistent seizures despite lamotrigine, levetiracetam, and oxcarbazepine, the findings are consistent with drug-resistant structural temporal lobe epilepsy.
