Chief Complaint: Follow-up generalized epilepsy with typical absence seizures

History of Present Illness:  
The patient is a 74-year-old woman with generalized epilepsy characterized by typical absence seizures, with onset at age 71. Her epilepsy has been attributed to a metabolic etiology. She remains on Vimpat (lacosamide) monotherapy; the current dose and frequency are documented in the reconciled medication record. This history is carried forward from prior neurology documentation. She was last seen on 12/28/2023 following review of an EEG report.

Since the last visit, she continues to experience typical absence seizures at an average frequency of approximately four per month per seizure calendar. Her epilepsy has demonstrated medication responsiveness, although she is not fully seizure-free. No interval emergency department visit or hospitalization for seizures is documented in the available record.

Her principal ongoing concern is the residual monthly seizure burden. We reviewed the importance of maintaining a seizure calendar and documenting possible provoking factors, including missed medication, sleep deprivation, intercurrent illness, and metabolic disturbance. Medication tolerability concerns were reviewed; no specific adverse effect is documented today. Quality-of-life impact is primarily related to continued unpredictable episodes of impaired awareness.
