**Recommendations:**

1. Discuss the epilepsy classification, genetic findings, seizure course, and incidental MRI lesion at PRC.  
2. Complete a comprehensive neuropsychological battery with formal performance-validity assessment and demographically corrected standardized scores.  
3. Review the MRI directly and clarify the location and clinical significance of the incidental lesion.  
4. Integrate prolonged video-EEG findings before considering any focal surgical intervention.  
5. If focal surgery is contemplated, obtain fMRI language mapping; consider Wada testing if fMRI is inconclusive or discordant with other data.  
6. Interpret processing-speed and word-retrieval findings in the context of topiramate treatment. Medication changes, if considered, should be directed by the treating epileptologist.  
7. Obtain psychiatric, educational, occupational, and functional histories and administer standardized mood measures.  
8. If an intervention is undertaken, repeat neuropsychological evaluation approximately 6–12 months afterward to assess cognitive outcome and guide rehabilitation.
