Medical Decision-Making (MDM): Presentation is most consistent with a breakthrough typical absence seizure in the setting of known drug-resistant generalized epilepsy. Semiology matches prior absence events, and there was no prodromal chest pain, palpitations, focal neurologic deficit, or persistent altered mental status. No evidence of status epilepticus, CNS infection, significant metabolic trigger, or traumatic complication.

Patient remained seizure-free and neurologically intact throughout ED observation. No Ativan (lorazepam) or IV antiseizure loading medication was indicated because the event had terminated and he returned to baseline. In view of extensive existing polytherapy, medication adjustments were deferred to his epilepsy specialist. He is appropriate for outpatient management after neurology consultation.
