Assessment / Impression

This is an adult patient with focal epilepsy beginning at age 21, with focal impaired-awareness seizures currently occurring approximately once per month. The epilepsy has been categorized as structural, although the available MRI report is normal and no causative lesion has been specified. Current ASMs are zonisamide and perampanel.

The referral information characterizes the epilepsy as drug responsive. At the same time, the patient continues to have approximately monthly seizures. These statements are not necessarily mutually exclusive, as treatment may have produced a clinically meaningful response without complete seizure freedom. However, the available history is insufficient to determine the degree of response, whether current doses are optimized, or whether the patient has previously failed two appropriately selected and tolerated ASM regimens. Thus, the patient should not be labeled as having drug-resistant epilepsy solely on the basis of the information available today.

Further diagnostic clarification should focus on confirming the habitual event description, reviewing prior EEG data, obtaining the original MRI images, and documenting the full ASM treatment history. If the seizures continue despite adequately dosed and tolerated therapy, VEEG monitoring and a more formal presurgical evaluation could be considered. At present, the supplied information does not establish candidacy for resective surgery, ablation, VNS, RNS, or DBS.
