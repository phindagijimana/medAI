MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Generalized epilepsy with typical absence seizures beginning at age 66, approximately three seizures per month; drug responsive on oxcarbazepine. Evaluation of suspected structural etiology/tumor.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. Postcontrast T1-weighted sequences were obtained following intravenous administration of gadolinium-based contrast.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles are normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, extra-axial collection, or midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma:

Supratentorial: Within the left superior frontal gyrus is a 2.3 x 1.8 x 1.7 cm expansile cortically based and subcortical lesion demonstrating T2/FLAIR hyperintensity and T1 hypointensity. There is mild localized gyral expansion without significant surrounding edema or mass effect. No associated restricted diffusion, susceptibility artifact, or abnormal enhancement. No additional intracranial mass is identified. No acute infarction or hemorrhage.

Infratentorial: No focal parenchymal signal abnormality or abnormal enhancement.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal volume loss or T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Aside from the left superior frontal lesion described above, cortical morphology and gyration are preserved. No gray matter heterotopia, polymicrogyria, focal cortical dysplasia, or other malformation of cortical development is identified.

White Matter: A few punctate supratentorial white matter FLAIR hyperintensities are nonspecific and within expected limits for age. Corpus callosum is normally formed.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature: Major intracranial arterial flow voids are preserved on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No suspicious marrow lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered paranasal sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Nonenhancing 2.3 cm expansile cortically based and subcortical lesion in the left superior frontal gyrus, favored to represent a low-grade glial or glioneuronal neoplasm. This may constitute a structural epileptogenic substrate. Neurosurgical consultation and imaging follow-up are recommended.
2. Normal appearance of the bilateral mesial temporal structures without evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.
3. No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, hydrocephalus, or significant mass effect.
