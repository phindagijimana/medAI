Emergency Department Provider Note

Chief Complaint: Breakthrough seizure.

HPI: 52-year-old male with drug-resistant focal epilepsy presents via ambulance after a witnessed breakthrough seizure. Per EMS report from xxx EMS, family observed behavioral arrest, impaired awareness, and repetitive hand movements consistent with his typical focal impaired-awareness seizures. He was confused afterward but progressively improved during transport. No recurrent seizure activity noted by EMS.

Medical Decision-Making (MDM): Presentation is most consistent with a breakthrough focal impaired-awareness seizure in a patient with known drug-resistant focal epilepsy. Differential included syncope, TIA, metabolic derangement, infection, medication toxicity/nonadherence, and psychogenic non-epileptic event. Typical semiology and post-ictal period favor epileptic seizure. No persistent deficit to suggest stroke/TIA, no infectious symptoms or meningismus, and no major metabolic trigger identified.

Patient was placed on seizure precautions and observed on cardiac and pulse-ox monitoring. No benzodiazepine or IV antiseizure load was indicated because the seizure had terminated before arrival, he returned to baseline, and there was no recurrence during ED observation. He tolerated PO and ambulated independently. No indication for emergent MRI, lumbar puncture, intubation, or admission at this time.

Assessment:  
1. Breakthrough focal impaired-awareness seizure.  
2. Drug-resistant focal epilepsy of unknown etiology.

Disposition: Discharged home in stable condition after neurology consultation. Continue lacosamide and carbamazepine exactly as prescribed; do not stop or double doses without neurology direction. Expedited epilepsy clinic follow-up advised. Seizure precautions reviewed, including no driving per state law, swimming alone, bathing unattended, climbing, or operating heavy machinery. Return immediately for seizure lasting >5 minutes, repeated seizures without recovery, new focal deficit, persistent confusion, fever, severe headache, injury, or other worsening symptoms.
