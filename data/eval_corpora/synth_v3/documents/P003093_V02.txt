MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Drug-resistant focal epilepsy with focal to bilateral tonic-clonic seizures, onset at age 69, with suspected structural etiology. Currently seizure-free (0 seizures/month) on topiramate, carbamazepine, and perampanel.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the temporal lobes, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. No intravenous contrast was administered.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles are within normal limits in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, midline shift, extra-axial collection, or significant intracranial mass effect.

Brain Parenchyma: Focal chronic supratentorial encephalomalacia and surrounding gliosis are present, compatible with remote parenchymal injury and potentially representing a structural epileptogenic substrate. No restricted diffusion to indicate acute infarction. No acute intracranial hemorrhage, mass lesion, or abnormal susceptibility deposition.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The bilateral hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal volume loss or abnormal T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis. The parahippocampal gyri and amygdalae are symmetric.

Cortex: No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, abnormal gyral pattern, or transmantle signal abnormality. No imaging evidence of focal cortical dysplasia, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: Minimal scattered punctate supratentorial T2/FLAIR hyperintensities are nonspecific and may reflect mild chronic microvascular ischemic change. No confluent white matter abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal in signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: No focal signal abnormality. Cerebellar volume is preserved. No inferior cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature: Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are maintained on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered paranasal sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: No acute orbital abnormality.

Impression:

1. Focal chronic supratentorial encephalomalacia with surrounding gliosis, compatible with remote structural injury and a potential substrate for focal epilepsy.
2. Normal appearance of the bilateral hippocampi. No mesial temporal sclerosis or identifiable malformation of cortical development.
3. No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or hydrocephalus.
