Indication: Drug-resistant focal epilepsy with focal-to-bilateral tonic-clonic seizures, clinically attributed to an infectious etiology. Seizure onset at age 10, currently approximately four episodes per week despite levetiracetam, perampanel, and carbamazepine. Evaluate for structural epileptogenic substrate.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin-section coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles are normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, midline shift, extra-axial collection, or abnormal extra-axial susceptibility.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass, or mass effect. No focal encephalomalacia or gliosis is identified. No abnormal mineralization or chronic blood products on susceptibility-weighted imaging.

Supratentorial: Normal cerebral volume and signal for age. No focal lesion is identified to account for the patient’s seizures.

Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality involving the brainstem or cerebellum.

Mesial Temporal Structures: The hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal T2/FLAIR hyperintensity or volume loss. The amygdalae and parahippocampal gyri are symmetric. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Gyral formation and cortical thickness are within normal limits. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle signal abnormality, heterotopia, polymicrogyria, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: No significant white matter signal abnormality. Corpus callosum is normally formed.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal in signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Cerebellar hemispheres and vermis are normally formed. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature: Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered paranasal sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: No significant abnormality on nondedicated imaging.

Impression:

1. No structural epileptogenic lesion identified.  
2. Symmetric, normal-appearing mesial temporal structures without evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.  
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
