Cortex: Normal cortical thickness and gyral pattern. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white matter junction, transmantle signal abnormality, heterotopic gray matter, polymicrogyria, or other malformation of cortical development.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Symmetric and normal in signal.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal morphology and signal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Minimal scattered paranasal sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: No significant abnormality on nondedicated imaging.
