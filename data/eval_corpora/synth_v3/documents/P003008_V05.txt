Chief Complaint: Follow-up focal epilepsy

History of Present Illness:

Brain MRI has demonstrated a vascular malformation felt to be the likely epileptogenic substrate. He remains on Fycompa (perampanel) 4 mg nightly as monotherapy. This history is carried forward from prior epilepsy clinic documentation.

He was last seen on 04/20/2026. Since that visit, he continues to experience an average of approximately three focal aware seizures per week. His seizure calendar was reviewed today and is consistent with this frequency. Awareness is retained during the events. He reports taking perampanel consistently, without an interval medication change. No interval emergency department evaluation or hospitalization for seizures was reported. He has not reported a new seizure type.

His principal concern today is the persistence of frequent seizures despite a clinically meaningful response to perampanel. The recurrent events remain disruptive to daily activities and quality of life. We reviewed the distinction between medication response and complete seizure freedom; his epilepsy is currently considered drug responsive, although not fully controlled. He is interested in further medication optimization while minimizing potential adverse effects.
