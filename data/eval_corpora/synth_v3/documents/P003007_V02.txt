MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 32, previously occurring approximately four times monthly; drug responsive on levetiracetam. Evaluate suspected structural etiology.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the temporal lobes, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, double inversion recovery, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. Post-contrast T1-weighted sequences were obtained following intravenous administration of gadolinium-based contrast.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles are normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus or extra-axial fluid collection. Basal cisterns are patent.

Brain Parenchyma:

Supratentorial: Centered within the left superior frontal gyrus is a cortically based, mildly expansile lesion measuring approximately 1.8 x 1.5 x 1.6 cm. The lesion demonstrates heterogeneous T2/FLAIR hyperintensity with small internal cystic-appearing components and T1 hypointensity. No associated restricted diffusion, susceptibility artifact, or appreciable enhancement. Minimal adjacent white matter T2/FLAIR signal abnormality is present without significant mass effect. Imaging appearance favors a low-grade epilepsy-associated neoplasm, including dysembryoplastic neuroepithelial tumor or ganglioglioma.

Infratentorial: No focal parenchymal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Bilateral hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal volume loss or abnormal T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Aside from the left superior frontal cortically based lesion, cortical morphology and gyration are preserved. No additional focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, heterotopia, polymicrogyria, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: No additional significant white matter signal abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal in signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No suspicious marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Approximately 1.8 cm nonenhancing cortically based lesion in the left superior frontal gyrus, favored to represent a low-grade epilepsy-associated tumor such as a dysembryoplastic neuroepithelial tumor or ganglioglioma. No significant mass effect.
2. Normal appearance of the mesial temporal structures without evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.
3. No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, hydrocephalus, or midline shift.
