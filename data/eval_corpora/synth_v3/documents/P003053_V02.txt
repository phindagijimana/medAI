Medical Decision-Making: Presentation most consistent with a breakthrough generalized tonic-clonic seizure in a child with known generalized, drug-resistant epilepsy. Assessment:  
1. Breakthrough generalized tonic-clonic seizure.  
2. Generalized drug-resistant epilepsy of unknown etiology.

Disposition: Discharge home with parent. Continue current ASMs exactly as prescribed; no ED medication changes. Follow up with pediatric epilepsy/Neurology within 1 week for reassessment and pending medication levels. Seizure precautions reviewed: constant supervision around water, no unsupervised bathing or swimming, avoid heights, and use protective measures during activities. Return immediately for seizure lasting >5 minutes, recurrent seizures without recovery, breathing difficulty, injury, fever with neck stiffness, persistent confusion, focal weakness, or other worsening symptoms.
