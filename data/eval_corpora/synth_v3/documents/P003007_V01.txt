Assessment / Impression

This patient has epilepsy beginning at age 32, with generalized tonic-clonic seizures reported at a frequency of approximately four per month. The epilepsy has been classified as generalized and structurally mediated due to a tumor identified on brain MRI. The patient is currently taking levetiracetam, and the epilepsy is described in the available records as drug responsive.

The available information is limited, particularly with respect to the imaging and electrophysiologic basis for the generalized classification. A tumor may be clinically relevant to seizure onset, but the actual MRI must be reviewed before determining whether it is likely to be causative, incidental, or potentially appropriate for surgical evaluation. Similarly, EEG data would be helpful to assess whether the electrographic pattern supports a generalized epilepsy or suggests focal onset with rapid bilateral spread.

The reported frequency of four generalized tonic-clonic seizures per month represents a clinically important ongoing seizure burden, even though the epilepsy has been characterized as medication responsive. Medication reconciliation is necessary because the current levetiracetam dose and schedule are unknown. At this stage, I would not label the patient as having drug-resistant epilepsy because the provided plan specifically identifies the condition as drug responsive, and no history of failed adequate ASM trials was supplied.
