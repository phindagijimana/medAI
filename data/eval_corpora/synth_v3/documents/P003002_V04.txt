Recommendations:  
1. Findings should be reviewed at PRC together with the complete epilepsy history, MRI images, scalp and/or intracranial EEG findings, and any available functional imaging.  
2. The complete neuropsychological score record, including performance-validity data, should be obtained before surgical cognitive risk is estimated.  
3. If surgery is under consideration, language laterality should be established with task-based fMRI. Wada testing may be considered if fMRI is technically limited, atypical, or discordant with other lateralizing data.  
4. Surgical counseling should address potential changes in verbal or visual memory only after seizure focus laterality, language dominance, and baseline material-specific memory abilities have been clarified.  
5. If an intervention is performed, repeat neuropsychological evaluation approximately 6–12 months afterward is recommended to document cognitive outcome and guide rehabilitation planning.
