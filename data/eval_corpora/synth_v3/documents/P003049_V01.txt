Assessment / Impression

This is an adult patient with generalized epilepsy of unknown etiology, presenting with generalized tonic-clonic seizures that began at age 30. The current seizure burden is approximately one generalized tonic-clonic seizure per year. The epilepsy is identified as drug resistant, with ongoing treatment using levetiracetam, brivaracetam, and oxcarbazepine.

Although the reported seizure frequency is relatively low, continued generalized tonic-clonic seizures remain clinically important. The currently available information is insufficient to determine how the generalized epilepsy classification was established or whether each current and prior ASM trial was adequate with respect to dose, duration, tolerability, and adherence. I would not recommend abrupt discontinuation of any ASM. Any adjustment should be made only after verification of the current regimen and review of prior treatment response.
