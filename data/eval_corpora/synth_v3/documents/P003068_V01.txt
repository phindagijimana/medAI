Assessment / Impression

This patient has epilepsy beginning at age 8, with habitual events described as nonmotor seizures of unknown onset. The epilepsy type remains unknown because the available history does not provide sufficient semiologic or EEG information to classify the condition as focal, generalized, or combined.

The reported hippocampal sclerosis supports a structural etiology. Such options would be reconsidered only if disabling seizures recur despite appropriately selected and tolerated medication trials.
