**Assessment and Plan:**  
1. **Generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures, structural etiology, drug-responsive**  
   - Seizure onset at age 11. Current reported frequency is approximately five seizures per week. Although he has shown medication responsiveness, the present seizure burden is clinically significant and requires further characterization and treatment optimization.  
   - Continue Keppra (levetiracetam) and Lamictal (lamotrigine) for now. Verify exact doses, dosing schedule, adherence, missed doses, and tolerability through medication reconciliation before making changes.

Discussed emergency evaluation for a convulsive seizure lasting five minutes or longer, repeated seizures without return to baseline, significant injury, respiratory compromise, or an event differing from his usual pattern.  
   - Reviewed the elevated risk associated with frequent generalized tonic-clonic seizures, including injury and SUDEP. Emphasized medication adherence, sleep regularity, and prompt communication for increased frequency or prolonged events.
