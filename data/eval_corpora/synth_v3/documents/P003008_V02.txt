Indication:  Focal epilepsy with focal aware seizures beginning at age 30, occurring approximately three times weekly; reported drug-responsive on perampanel. Evaluation for structural seizure focus.

Comparison:  None.

Technique:  Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the temporal lobes, diffusion-weighted imaging with ADC maps, susceptibility-weighted imaging, double inversion recovery imaging, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces:  Ventricles are normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, extra-axial collection, or midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma:

Supratentorial:  A small compact cluster of serpiginous vascular signal voids is present in the supratentorial brain, with associated susceptibility artifact, compatible with a vascular malformation. There is no surrounding vasogenic edema, significant mass effect, or evidence of acute hemorrhage. No acute infarct or intracranial mass.

Infratentorial:  No focal parenchymal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures:  The hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal volume loss or T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. No evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex:  Cortical thickness and gyral pattern are preserved. No focal cortical dysplasia, polymicrogyria, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development is identified.

White Matter:  No significant white matter signal abnormality. Corpus callosum is normally formed.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami:  Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum:  Normal signal and volume. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature:  Major intracranial arterial flow voids are preserved. Vascular malformation as described above is incompletely characterized on this nonangiographic examination.

Skull Base and Calvarium:  No suspicious marrow signal abnormality.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells:  Mild scattered paranasal sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits:  No gross abnormality.

Impression:

1. Supratentorial vascular malformation with associated susceptibility artifact, providing a potential structural substrate for focal epilepsy. No surrounding edema, acute hemorrhage, or significant mass effect. Dedicated contrast-enhanced MRI and intracranial MRA or CTA may be considered for further characterization if not previously performed.
2. Normal and symmetric mesial temporal structures without evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis.
3. No acute infarct, intracranial mass, hydrocephalus, or midline shift.
