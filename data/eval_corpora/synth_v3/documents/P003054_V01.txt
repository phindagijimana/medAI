Assessment / Impression

This patient has generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 11. The reported seizure burden is approximately five events per week. The epilepsy is described as structurally mediated in the setting of an MRI-identified vascular malformation.

The patient is currently receiving dual-ASM therapy with levetiracetam and lamotrigine. The supplied phenotype classifies the epilepsy as drug responsive. Because the stated frequency remains high, it will be important to clarify whether the reported five weekly events represent the current treated frequency, a historical frequency, or another point in the clinical course. Medication adherence, exact doses, and response to each regimen were not available for review today and should not be presumed.

The vascular malformation requires direct imaging review. A generalized electroclinical presentation does not, by itself, establish that the MRI lesion is responsible for the seizures. Conversely, a structural lesion may be clinically important even when the reported seizure type is generalized. Correlation among semiology, EEG findings, and lesion anatomy is therefore necessary before considering any lesion-directed or surgical evaluation.
