Vasculature: Expected major intracranial arterial flow voids are maintained.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered mucosal thickening in the ethmoid air cells. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: No gross orbital abnormality.
