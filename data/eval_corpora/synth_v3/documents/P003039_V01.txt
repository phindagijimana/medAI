Assessment / Impression

This is an adult patient with epilepsy beginning at 9 years of age and generalized tonic-clonic seizures reported at a frequency of approximately three per week. The provided epilepsy classification is generalized epilepsy with a structural etiology, and MRI reportedly demonstrates focal cortical dysplasia. The patient is currently taking valproate and levetiracetam, with the regimen described as drug responsive.

The principal diagnostic issue is the apparent discordance between the generalized epilepsy classification and the focal structural MRI lesion. Focal cortical dysplasia may be clinically relevant, but an MRI lesion should not be assumed to be the seizure onset zone without electroclinical correlation. Conversely, the lesion may be incidental to a generalized epilepsy syndrome. Prior EEG data, direct MRI review, and a more detailed event history are needed to distinguish these possibilities.

Although the epilepsy is identified as drug responsive, a seizure burden of three generalized tonic-clonic seizures per week warrants prompt clarification. We need to determine whether this frequency reflects the current treated burden, a pretreatment baseline, or incomplete response. Based on the information provided, I would not yet relabel the epilepsy as drug resistant or recommend resective epilepsy surgery. If frequent seizures are ongoing despite verified, adequately dosed medication trials, inpatient EMU evaluation and a formal multidisciplinary epilepsy surgery workup may become appropriate.

Recommendations / Plan

1. Continue valproate and levetiracetam without an empiric change until the exact formulations, doses, frequencies, adherence, and prior response are confirmed. The patient or referring office should provide an updated medication list and pharmacy information.

2. Obtain the original brain MRI images for review at xxx Medical Center. If the prior study is not an epilepsy-protocol MRI or is technically inadequate, we will consider repeat imaging using a dedicated epilepsy protocol.
