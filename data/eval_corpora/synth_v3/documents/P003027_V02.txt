Indication:  Drug-resistant focal epilepsy with focal aware seizures beginning at age 66, currently approximately two seizures per year despite lamotrigine, levetiracetam, and oxcarbazepine. Evaluate for structural epileptogenic substrate.

Comparison:  None.

Technique:  Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain was performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces:  Ventricles and sulci are within normal limits for age. No hydrocephalus or extra-axial collection. No midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma:

Supratentorial:  No acute infarct, intracranial hemorrhage, mass, or significant mass effect. No abnormal restricted diffusion or pathologic susceptibility artifact.

Infratentorial:  No focal abnormality within the brainstem or cerebellum.

Mesial Temporal Structures:  The left hippocampal volume is subjectively decreased compared with the right, with left-right asymmetry of approximately 20%. There is associated increased T2/FLAIR signal and loss of normal internal hippocampal architecture. Mild prominence of the left temporal horn is present on an ex vacuo basis. The right hippocampus is normal in volume and signal with preserved internal architecture. No mesial temporal mass.

Cortex:  No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, abnormal gyration, or transmantle signal abnormality to suggest focal cortical dysplasia or other malformation of cortical development. No heterotopic gray matter.

White Matter:  Minimal scattered punctate supratentorial T2/FLAIR hyperintensities are nonspecific and within expected limits for age. No confluent white matter lesion.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami:  Normal in signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum:  Normal cerebellar volume and foliation. No focal lesion.

Vasculature:  Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved.

Skull Base and Calvarium:  No suspicious marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells:  Mild mucosal thickening in the maxillary sinuses. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits:  No acute abnormality.

Impression:

1. Left mesial temporal sclerosis, characterized by hippocampal volume loss, increased T2/FLAIR signal, and loss of internal architecture.
2. No additional structural epileptogenic lesion or malformation of cortical development identified.
3. No acute intracranial hemorrhage, infarct, mass, or hydrocephalus.
