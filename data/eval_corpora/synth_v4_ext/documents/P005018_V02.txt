Medical Decision-Making:  
Presentation most consistent with breakthrough generalized tonic-clonic seizure in a patient with known drug-resistant combined generalized and focal epilepsy. Current event matches established semiology: no aura, impaired awareness, tonic stiffening followed by clonic jerking. No evidence of status epilepticus. He is afebrile, hemodynamically stable, and back to baseline with a nonfocal exam. No clear metabolic, infectious, toxic, or adherence-related trigger identified.

Differential considered included epileptic seizure, syncope, TIA, psychogenic nonepileptic event, electrolyte derangement, hypoglycemia, and intracranial process. Witnessed motor pattern and post-ictal period favor epileptic seizure. Low concern for meningitis, stroke, or acute intracranial injury. Given reassuring workup, no recurrence during observation, and Neurology agreement, admission or emergent EEG is not indicated today.
