Indication: H/o focal aware epilepsy beginning at age 42, with events reported up to 3/week characterized by emotional onset, hand tingling, and clonic jerking with preserved awareness. Video EEG monitoring demonstrated normal background with right parietal interictal epileptiform discharges and right parietal ictal onset. Structural etiology suspected; drug responsive on lacosamide. r/o epileptogenic lesion.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner without IV contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, DIR, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus or extra-axial collection. No midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma: Small focal region of cortical and subcortical encephalomalacia in the right inferior parietal lobule, with associated volume loss and surrounding T2/FLAIR hyperintense gliosis. No restricted diffusion, mass effect, or associated susceptibility artifact. No acute infarct, intracranial hemorrhage, or mass lesion.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Bilateral hippocampi symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal T2/FLAIR hyperintensity or volume loss. No mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Aside from the right parietal encephalomalacia, cortical morphology and gyral pattern are preserved. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: No additional significant white matter signal abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal signal and volume. No focal infratentorial lesion.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No suspicious marrow signal abnormality.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Focal right inferior parietal cortical/subcortical encephalomalacia with surrounding gliosis, providing a potential structural substrate for the reported right parietal seizure onset.
2. Normal appearance of the bilateral hippocampi. No mesial temporal sclerosis or malformation of cortical development.
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
