Assessment / Impression

This is an XX-year-old patient with focal epilepsy beginning at age 6. The habitual events consist of emotional nonmotor onset followed by impaired awareness and tonic posturing, clinically consistent with focal impaired-awareness seizures. The described events are possibly focal onset; definitive localization cannot be made from the available semiology and routine EEG alone.

The routine EEG is abnormal because of focal slowing and right occipital IEDs, supporting focal cerebral dysfunction and an increased propensity for focal seizures. However, no habitual event was captured, and there is no documented ictal onset. The MRI shows nonspecific white matter changes without an identified etiology. The etiology of the focal epilepsy therefore remains unknown.

The patient reports approximately seven events per week while taking carbamazepine and perampanel. These details must be clarified before determining whether treatment adjustment is indicated.
