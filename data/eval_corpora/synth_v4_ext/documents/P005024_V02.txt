Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Focal epilepsy with onset at age 25, characterized by impaired awareness with cognitive onset, tonic posturing, and evolution to focal-to-bilateral tonic-clonic seizures; reported frequency approximately 3/month. Events are possibly focal onset seizures and have been described as drug-responsive on lamotrigine and oxcarbazepine. Video EEG demonstrated left temporal slowing, left temporal interictal epileptiform discharges, and left temporal ictal onset. No h/o head trauma. Family hx notable for maternal stroke. Evaluate for structural seizure focus; r/o mesial temporal sclerosis.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal T2/FLAIR images through the temporal lobes, DIR, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. No intravenous contrast was administered.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles and sulci are within normal limits for age. No hydrocephalus, extra-axial collection, or midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or vasogenic edema. No abnormal susceptibility artifact.

Supratentorial: No focal parenchymal lesion beyond the mesial temporal abnormality described below.

Infratentorial: No focal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Left hippocampal volume is subjectively decreased compared with the right, with left-right asymmetry of approximately 20%. Associated mild T2/FLAIR hyperintensity and blurring/loss of normal left hippocampal internal architecture. Mild prominence of the adjacent left temporal horn. The right hippocampus is normal in volume and signal with preserved internal architecture. Findings are consistent with probable left mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Normal gyral and sulcal pattern. No focal cortical thickening, gray-white junction blurring, transmantle sign, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: No significant white matter signal abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal signal and volume. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature: Preserved major intracranial vascular flow voids on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Findings consistent with probable left mesial temporal sclerosis, including decreased left hippocampal volume, T2/FLAIR hyperintensity, and loss of internal architecture. This is concordant with the reported left temporal ictal onset on video EEG.
2. No additional structural epileptogenic lesion or malformation of cortical development.
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
