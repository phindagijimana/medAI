Routine Electroencephalogram Report

Study Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx  
Indication: H/o focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures occurring approximately once weekly; evaluate for interictal epileptiform activity and r/o electrographic seizures.

Technique: A 30-minute digital EEG was performed using the international 10-20 system with 21 scalp electrodes and a single-lead ECG. Standard activation procedures included hyperventilation for 3 minutes and intermittent photic stimulation. The patient was awake and drowsy during the recording. Stage N2 sleep was achieved. This was not a video EEG monitoring admission.

Clinical History: Seizure onset occurred at age 54. Typical events begin with a focal nonmotor emotional sensation and déjà vu aura, followed by impaired awareness. Spasm-like motor activity, described clinically as epileptic spasms, may accompany the events. Current frequency is approximately one seizure per week. Etiology remains unknown. Epilepsy has been clinically drug responsive. Current maintenance ASMs are levetiracetam and oxcarbazepine. Lacosamide was previously trialed and discontinued due to GI intolerance. No h/o developmental delay.

Description:

- **Background:** The awake background was continuous, symmetric, and appropriately organized. A 9-10 Hz posterior dominant rhythm was present bilaterally, reactive to eye opening and closure. An expected anterior-to-posterior gradient was maintained. Low-amplitude beta activity was present anteriorly. No persistent focal or generalized slowing was identified.

- **Awake state:** Normal admixture of alpha and beta frequencies. No significant interhemispheric asymmetry.

- **Drowsy/sleep:** Drowsiness was associated with attenuation of the PDR and emergence of symmetric theta activity. Stage N2 sleep contained appropriately formed, symmetric vertex waves, sleep spindles, and K complexes.

- **Hyperventilation:** HV produced mild symmetric background slowing without activation of epileptiform abnormalities.

- **Photic stimulation:** PS at sequential flash frequencies produced a symmetric posterior driving response. No photoparoxysmal response was seen.

- **Interictal findings:** Occasional medium-amplitude sharp waves were present over the left temporal region, maximal at F7/T7 with a field extending to FT9. These discharges were more apparent during drowsiness and sleep. No independent right-sided epileptiform discharges, generalized spike-wave discharges, LPDs, GPDs, or TIRDA were identified.

- **Ictal events:** No clinical events or electrographic seizures were captured. Accordingly, ictal onset and electroclinical localization cannot be determined from this routine study.

- **ECG:** Single-lead tracing demonstrated a regular rhythm.

Impression:

1. Abnormal routine EEG due to occasional left temporal sharp waves, maximal over the left anterior-to-mid temporal region. These IEDs indicate increased epileptogenic potential in the left temporal region and support the clinical diagnosis of focal epilepsy.
2. Otherwise normal awake and sleep background without focal slowing or diffuse encephalopathy.
3. No electrographic seizures were recorded. The absence of a captured seizure precludes determination of ictal onset. Clinical correlation is recommended for the reported focal nonmotor emotional onset, déjà vu aura, impaired awareness, and associated spasm-like motor manifestations.
