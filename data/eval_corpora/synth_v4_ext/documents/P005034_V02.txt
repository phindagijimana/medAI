MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Focal epilepsy with focal-to-bilateral tonic-clonic seizures, onset at age 42. Events approximately 3/week, with epigastric rising aura followed by impaired awareness, epileptic spasms, and tonic posturing, consistent with focal autonomic and motor onset. Prolonged EEG demonstrated a normal background and left temporal interictal epileptiform discharges; no ictal onset recorded. Reportedly drug responsive on topiramate; clonazepam orally disintegrating tablet PRN for rescue. No h/o head trauma. Evaluate for structural seizure focus and r/o mesial temporal sclerosis.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial FLAIR, thin-section coronal T2 and FLAIR through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles and sulci are within normal limits for age. No hydrocephalus, extra-axial collection, or midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or abnormal susceptibility. No focal abnormality within the infratentorial brain.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Subjectively decreased left hippocampal volume compared with the right, with left-right asymmetry of approximately 20%. Associated increased T2/FLAIR signal and blurring/loss of normal internal hippocampal architecture. Mild prominence of the left temporal horn. The right hippocampus is normal in volume and signal with preserved internal architecture.

Cortex: Normal gyral and sulcal pattern. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: No significant white matter signal abnormality. Corpus callosum is normally formed.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature: Preserved major intracranial arterial flow voids on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Left mesial temporal sclerosis, characterized by decreased left hippocampal volume, increased T2/FLAIR signal, and loss of internal architecture. This provides a structural correlate for the reported left temporal interictal epileptiform discharges.
2. No cortical malformation, acute intracranial abnormality, or additional structural epileptogenic lesion.
3. Mild ethmoid sinus mucosal disease.
