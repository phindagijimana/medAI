Neurology Progress Note

Chief Complaint: Follow-up combined generalized and focal epilepsy

History of Present Illness:  
This is an 18-year-old man with h/o combined generalized and focal epilepsy of unknown etiology, with seizure onset at age 12, returning for routine follow-up. His habitual generalized tonic-clonic seizures occur without aura and are described as tonic stiffening followed by clonic jerking with impaired awareness. He remains on Zonegran (zonisamide) and Fycompa (perampanel). Prior routine EEG demonstrated focal slowing with left temporal interictal epileptiform discharges.

Since the last visit, he reports no breakthrough seizures, with current seizure frequency 0/month per seizure calendar. He specifically denies any interval loss of consciousness. No recent medication changes or seizure-related ED visits. He reports taking both ASMs consistently and does not identify a new intolerable adverse effect today.

Overall, he feels that seizure control is good and has no new neurologic concern. Etiology remains unknown. A possible autoimmune contribution has previously been considered, although this remains unproven. Sleep, medication adherence, and avoidance of known seizure-provoking factors were reviewed.
