Medical Decision-Making (MDM):

Differential included epileptic seizure, syncope/dysrhythmia, TIA, metabolic derangement, infection, intoxication, and psychogenic non-epileptic event. Typical déjà vu aura, focal motor automatisms, tonic posturing, impaired awareness, and postictal confusion strongly support his usual focal seizure. No persistent deficit to suggest TIA/CVA. EKG and labs reassuring.

No fever, meningismus, trauma, prolonged seizure, repeated seizures, or acute CT abnormality. No Ativan or IV second-line ASM required in ED. Observed without recurrence; tolerating PO and ambulatory. Given baseline frequency of approximately two seizures/month and neurology recommendation, admission unlikely to provide benefit today.

Assessment:  
1. Breakthrough focal impaired-awareness seizure.  
2. Drug-resistant structural focal epilepsy associated with hippocampal sclerosis.  
3. Resolved postictal confusion.

Disposition:  
Discharge home in stable condition with responsible adult. Continue carbamazepine and perampanel without missed doses. Lorazepam SL PRN per existing rescue plan. Neurology/epilepsy clinic follow-up within 1 week.

Seizure precautions reviewed: no driving per applicable state law and neurology clearance, no swimming/bathing alone, heights, open flames, or operating heavy machinery. Return immediately for seizure lasting >5 minutes, repeated seizures without recovery, injury, persistent confusion, new weakness, fever, severe headache, or other worsening symptoms.
