Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner using an epilepsy protocol. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and thin-section coronal FLAIR through the temporal lobes, DIR, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. No intravenous contrast administered.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles and sulci within normal limits for age. No hydrocephalus, extra-axial collection, or midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarct, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or vasogenic edema. No restricted diffusion or abnormal susceptibility artifact.

Supratentorial: Focal cortical thickening with blurring of the gray-white matter junction and subtle adjacent subcortical T2/FLAIR hyperintense signal, compatible with focal cortical dysplasia. No associated restricted diffusion, hemorrhage, or mass effect.

Infratentorial: No focal parenchymal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Bilateral hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal volume loss or abnormal T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. No mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Focal cortical dysplasia as described above. Otherwise preserved gyral pattern. No additional heterotopia, polymicrogyria, or other malformation of cortical development identified.

White Matter: No additional significant white matter signal abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature: Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered paranasal sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Focal cortical dysplasia, characterized by focal cortical thickening, gray-white junction blurring, and subtle subcortical T2/FLAIR hyperintensity; potential structural epileptogenic substrate.
2. Normal appearance of the bilateral hippocampi. No mesial temporal sclerosis.
3. No acute intracranial hemorrhage, infarct, mass effect, or hydrocephalus.
