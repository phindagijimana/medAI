MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Structural generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 37, currently approximately 7/week and reportedly drug responsive on carbamazepine. Events characterized by impaired awareness and tonic stiffening followed by clonic jerking, without aura. Routine EEG demonstrated a normal background with bilateral generalized interictal epileptiform discharges; no ictal onset recorded. No h/o febrile seizures in childhood. Evaluate known focal cortical dysplasia and r/o additional epileptogenic lesion.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. No intravenous contrast administered.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles and sulci within normal limits for age. No hydrocephalus, midline shift, or extra-axial collection.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or vasogenic edema. No restricted diffusion or abnormal susceptibility artifact.

Supratentorial/Cortex: Focal cortical thickening with blurring of the gray-white matter junction and subtle subjacent radial T2/FLAIR hyperintensity extending toward the lateral ventricular margin, compatible with focal cortical dysplasia. No associated restricted diffusion, hemorrhage, or mass effect. No additional cortical malformation, polymicrogyria, heterotopia, or encephalocele identified.

Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Bilateral hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture and normal morphology. No hippocampal volume loss or T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. No mesial temporal sclerosis.

White Matter: No additional significant white matter signal abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature: Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are maintained on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered paranasal sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Imaging findings compatible with focal cortical dysplasia, including focal cortical thickening, gray-white junction blurring, and subtle radial T2/FLAIR hyperintensity.  
2. No mesial temporal sclerosis or additional structural epileptogenic lesion identified.  
3. No acute intracranial abnormality. If subsequent MRI demonstrates progression, PET/SPECT may be obtained to assess concordance.
