Assessment / Impression

This is an adult patient with focal epilepsy beginning at age 66 in the setting of an LGI1 antibody-associated immune etiology. The habitual semiology consists of an epigastric rising aura followed by versive head turning and clonic jerking with preserved awareness. These are focal aware seizures with focal motor onset. There is no post-ictal confusion.

The patient continues to have approximately three seizures per year while taking three maintenance ASMs and after prior trials of valproate, zonisamide, levetiracetam, and oxcarbazepine. The patient is also s/p right LITT in 2017. This presentation is therefore consistent with probable, likely drug-resistant focal epilepsy.

The routine EEG demonstrated focal slowing but no epileptiform discharges and did not establish an ictal onset zone. MRI reportedly showed an incidental lesion, but the relationship of that finding to the epilepsy cannot be determined without review of the images. Additional electroclinical characterization would be appropriate before recommending medication changes, repeat ablation, resection, or neuromodulation.
