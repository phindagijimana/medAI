Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Bilateral hippocampi are symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture and normal morphology. No hippocampal volume loss or T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. No mesial temporal sclerosis.

Orbits: Unremarkable.
