Indication: Drug-resistant focal epilepsy with h/o focal aware sensory-onset seizures since age 19, possibly focal onset seizures with preserved awareness, hyperkinetic movements, and tonic posturing. Currently seizure-free. Evaluate structural etiology and r/o additional epileptogenic lesion.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. No intravenous contrast administered.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles and sulci within normal limits for age. No hydrocephalus, extra-axial collection, or midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarct, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or vasogenic edema. No restricted diffusion or abnormal susceptibility artifact.

Supratentorial: No focal parenchymal mass or encephalomalacia. Gray-white matter differentiation is maintained.

Infratentorial: No focal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Asymmetric hippocampal volume loss, estimated at approximately 20% relative to the opposite side, with associated T2/FLAIR hyperintensity and loss of normal internal hippocampal architecture. Mild prominence of the adjacent temporal horn is present on the affected side. Findings are consistent with mesial temporal sclerosis. The opposite hippocampus maintains normal signal and internal architecture. No mesial temporal mass.

Cortex: Normal gyral and sulcal pattern. No cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, polymicrogyria, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: No significant white matter signal abnormality. Corpus callosum is normally formed.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Expected intracranial arterial flow voids are maintained.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered paranasal sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Hippocampal volume loss, T2/FLAIR hyperintensity, and loss of internal architecture, consistent with mesial temporal sclerosis/hippocampal sclerosis.  
2. No additional epileptogenic structural lesion or malformation of cortical development identified.  
3. No acute intracranial hemorrhage, infarct, mass effect, or hydrocephalus.
