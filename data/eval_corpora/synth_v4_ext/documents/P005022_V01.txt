Neurology Progress Note

**Chief Complaint:** Follow-up focal epilepsy

**History of Present Illness:**  
The patient is a 23-year-old woman with h/o focal epilepsy of unknown etiology, with seizure onset at age 10. Typical events are focal impaired-awareness seizures. She describes visual phenomena at onset, followed by an autonomic sensation and impaired awareness, with tonic posturing during the event. She remains on Lamictal (lamotrigine) and Briviact (brivaracetam) as maintenance therapy. Her epilepsy has been drug responsive, although she continues to have infrequent breakthrough seizures.

She reports an average seizure frequency of approximately 2 seizures/year per seizure diary. Exact dates of the most recent events were not available for review today. No definite lateralizing features have been established. Prior ambulatory EEG showed mild diffuse background slowing without epileptiform discharges; no electrographic seizures or ictal onset were identified. There is no h/o developmental delay or head trauma.

Current concerns remain prevention of additional breakthrough seizures and maintaining safety and independence. Medication reconciliation was completed. Previously trialed Depakote (valproate), discontinued because of inadequate seizure control, and Fycompa (perampanel), discontinued because of sedation. Nayzilam (midazolam) intranasal is available as rescue medication PRN for a prolonged seizure or seizure cluster.
