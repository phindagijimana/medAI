Neurology Progress Note

**Chief Complaint:** Follow-up epilepsy

**History of Present Illness:**  
This is a 94-year-old woman with epilepsy of unknown type, characterized by unknown-onset tonic-clonic seizures, who returns for routine follow-up. Per prior notes, seizures began at age 69 and are felt to have a structural etiology in the setting of a vascular malformation on brain MRI. She remains on Zonegran (zonisamide) as maintenance antiseizure therapy. Her epilepsy has been drug responsive.

Typical events occur without aura. Witnesses describe impaired awareness with tonic stiffening followed by clonic jerking. No definite focal onset or reliable lateralizing feature has been established. Seizure calendar reviewed today; she reports an average frequency of approximately two seizures per year. Exact dates were not available for review. No recent medication adjustment is documented.

Prior video EEG monitoring showed mild diffuse background slowing. No interictal epileptiform discharges were seen, and no electrographic seizures or localizable ictal onset were recorded. Today’s visit focused on continued seizure prevention, medication monitoring, and surveillance of the known vascular malformation.
