Neurology Progress Note

Chief Complaint: Follow-up generalized epilepsy

History of Present Illness:

This is a 57-year-old man with h/o adult-onset generalized epilepsy, presumed secondary to a remote infectious etiology, who returns for routine follow-up. Prior epilepsy documentation records seizure onset at age 58. His habitual seizures are generalized tonic-clonic events: no aura, followed by impaired awareness with tonic stiffening and subsequent clonic jerking. He remains on Briviact (brivaracetam) and Tegretol (carbamazepine), unchanged from the prior visit.

Interval seizure calendar reviewed. He reports no breakthrough seizures since the last visit, with current frequency 0/month. No medication changes were made in the interval. His epilepsy remains drug responsive on the present combination. He has Ativan (lorazepam) available SL PRN as rescue medication.

Prior routine EEG showed a normal background with bilateral generalized interictal epileptiform discharges; no ictal event was recorded. Overall presentation is most consistent with generalized epilepsy, although possibly focal-onset seizures with rapid bilateral spread cannot be excluded. His primary goal is continued seizure freedom without changing a currently effective regimen.
