Assessment / Impression

This patient has focal epilepsy with focal aware seizures beginning at age 13. The habitual semiology consists of an epigastric rising aura and emotional nonmotor onset followed by hyperkinetic movements, with awareness preserved. MRI demonstrated focal cortical dysplasia, making this a structural focal epilepsy.

The patient has a hx of drug-resistant epilepsy based on failure or intolerance of multiple ASMs and is s/p left laser interstitial thermal therapy in 2023. Despite the prior DRE designation, the patient is currently seizure-free at 0 seizures per month on combination therapy with oxcarbazepine, brivaracetam, and topiramate.

The routine EEG showed focal slowing without IEDs. A routine study without epileptiform discharges does not exclude focal epilepsy, particularly given the established clinical semiology, structural lesion, and prior surgical treatment.

At present, there is no indication to change an effective regimen until the exact doses, frequencies, tolerability, and adherence have been verified. If seizures recur, repeat presurgical evaluation may be appropriate. Depending on updated localization and the relationship of the epileptogenic network to the prior ablation, future options could include additional targeted therapy or neuromodulation such as the NeuroPace RNS System.
