Assessment / Impression

This adult patient has generalized epilepsy of genetic etiology, with onset at age 30. The primary seizure type is myoclonic: brief shock-like jerks of both arms on waking, with preserved awareness and no aura. Events currently occur approximately four times per week. The reported semiology and routine EEG showing bilateral generalized epileptiform discharges on an otherwise normal background are concordant with a generalized epilepsy phenotype.
