Neurology Progress Note

Chief Complaint: Follow-up drug-resistant focal epilepsy

History of Present Illness:  
The patient is a 49-year-old man with h/o focal epilepsy beginning at age 43, felt to be structural in etiology in the setting of hippocampal sclerosis. As documented in prior notes, his habitual seizures are focal impaired-awareness events. He may experience déjà vu followed by impaired awareness, automatisms, and tonic posturing. In ILAE terms, these are focal-onset motor seizures with automatisms. Current maintenance ASMs are Tegretol (carbamazepine) and Fycompa (perampanel).

He continues to have approximately 2 seizures/month per seizure calendar. Exact dates were not available for review today. The events remain similar to his established semiology without a clearly described new event type. A possible atypical absence phenotype has previously been raised and cannot be excluded, although the déjà vu aura, automatisms, tonic posturing, structural MRI abnormality, and right temporal interictal discharges favor focal epilepsy. Further event characterization may be needed to r/o atypical absence if the clinical pattern changes.

Prior routine EEG showed mild diffuse background slowing with right temporal interictal epileptiform discharges; no ictal onset was captured. MRI demonstrated hippocampal sclerosis. He has previously trialed lacosamide, discontinued due to rash; lamotrigine, discontinued due to cognitive side effects; and oxcarbazepine, discontinued due to mood side effects. Given ongoing seizures despite multiple appropriately selected ASMs, his epilepsy meets criteria for drug-resistant epilepsy. Ongoing impaired-awareness seizures remain clinically significant from a safety and quality-of-life standpoint.
