MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Focal epilepsy with focal aware motor seizures beginning at age 41, currently approximately 4/month. Events characterized by epileptic spasms and hyperkinetic movements with preserved awareness and no aura. Structural etiology suspected; r/o epileptogenic lesion. Routine EEG demonstrated normal background with right temporal interictal epileptiform discharges and no recorded ictal onset. Drug-responsive on lamotrigine and topiramate; previously trialed oxcarbazepine, discontinued due to GI intolerance. Intranasal midazolam PRN rescue medication.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner using an epilepsy protocol. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal images through the mesial temporal structures, DIR, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. No intravenous contrast administered.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles and sulci within normal limits for age. No hydrocephalus, extra-axial collection, or midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarction, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or vasogenic edema. No restricted diffusion or abnormal susceptibility.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Right hippocampal volume is subjectively decreased compared with the left, with right-left asymmetry of approximately 20%. Associated increased T2/FLAIR signal and loss of normal internal hippocampal architecture. Mild prominence of the right temporal horn. The left hippocampus is normal in volume and signal with preserved internal architecture.

Cortex: Normal gyral and sulcal pattern. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: No significant white matter signal abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal. No focal infratentorial lesion.

Vasculature: Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are maintained on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Findings consistent with right hippocampal sclerosis, including decreased hippocampal volume, T2/FLAIR hyperintensity, and loss of internal architecture.
2. This focal structural lesion is likely epileptogenic and concordant with the reported right temporal interictal epileptiform discharges.
3. No acute intracranial hemorrhage, infarction, mass effect, or additional structural seizure focus identified.
