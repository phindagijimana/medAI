Awake state: The awake background was continuous and appropriately reactive. No focal attenuation, breach rhythm, or pathologic delta activity was present.

Drowsy/Sleep: Drowsiness was characterized by attenuation of the PDR, slow rolling eye movements, and emergence of diffuse theta activity. Stage N2 sleep was marked by symmetric vertex waves, sleep spindles, and K complexes. Sleep architecture was normal and bilaterally synchronous.

Hyperventilation: HV produced mild, symmetric generalized theta slowing without focal activation or epileptiform discharges. The background returned promptly to baseline after cessation of the maneuver.

Photic stimulation: PS at frequencies from 1 to 30 Hz produced symmetric photic driving, best developed over O1/O2. There was no photoparoxysmal response.

Interictal findings: No definite spikes, sharp waves, spike-and-wave discharges, TIRDA, LPDs, or GPDs were identified. Specifically, there was no convincing temporal epileptiform activity at F7/T7 or F8/T8. No electrographic pattern suggestive of subclinical seizure activity was present.

Ictal events: No electrographic seizures or clinical events were captured. No push-button events occurred.

ECG: Single-channel ECG demonstrated a regular rhythm.
