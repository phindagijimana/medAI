Neurology Progress Note

Chief Complaint: Follow-up drug-resistant focal epilepsy

History of Present Illness:

46-year-old woman with hx of structural focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures, onset at age 33, currently treated with Topamax (topiramate) and Briviact (brivaracetam). Prior imaging demonstrated a focal cortical dysplasia characterized as Palmini type IIa. There is also a probable abnormality consistent with mesial temporal sclerosis. Epilepsy has remained drug resistant despite appropriate ASM trials.

Since the last assessment, seizure burden has remained approximately 2 focal impaired-awareness seizures per year. Patient reports 2 breakthrough seizures over the past year per seizure diary; exact dates were not available for review today. No definite photosensitive component, and she specifically denies photosensitivity. She remains on topiramate and brivaracetam. Previously trialed lacosamide, discontinued due to inadequate seizure control, and zonisamide, discontinued due to cognitive side effects.

Her primary concern remains the unpredictability of breakthrough seizures and their effect on independence and daily planning. We reviewed the importance of continuing a written or electronic seizure calendar and documenting possible provoking factors, duration, recovery time, and missed medication doses.
