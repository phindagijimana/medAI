MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures, onset at age 75 and reported frequency approximately 4/week; suspected structural etiology. Described as drug responsive on carbamazepine and valproate. Previously trialed perampanel, discontinued s/p rash. No h/o family epilepsy. Evaluate for structural seizure focus and r/o mesial temporal sclerosis.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin-section coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles and sulci within expected limits for age. No hydrocephalus, extra-axial collection, or midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarct, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or vasogenic edema. No restricted diffusion or abnormal susceptibility artifact. No focal infratentorial parenchymal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Asymmetric hippocampal volume loss, approximately 20% side-to-side, with corresponding T2/FLAIR hyperintensity and loss of normal internal hippocampal architecture in the smaller hippocampus. Mild prominence of the adjacent temporal horn. Findings are consistent with hippocampal sclerosis. No additional mesial temporal lesion is identified.

Cortex: Normal gyral and sulcal pattern. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: Mild scattered supratentorial white matter T2/FLAIR hyperintensities, nonspecific but likely reflecting chronic microvascular ischemic change. No confluent or tumefactive lesion.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Preserved signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: No focal abnormality. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature: Major intracranial arterial flow voids are maintained on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Minimal mucosal thickening in the ethmoid air cells. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: No acute abnormality on nondedicated imaging.

Impression:

1. Findings consistent with hippocampal sclerosis, including asymmetric hippocampal atrophy, T2/FLAIR hyperintensity, and loss of internal architecture. This provides a structural substrate for focal epilepsy.
2. Mild chronic microvascular ischemic white matter change.
3. No acute infarct, hemorrhage, intracranial mass, hydrocephalus, or additional epileptogenic structural lesion.
4. If subsequent MRI demonstrates progression, PET/SPECT may be obtained for concordance. Should seizure recurrence occur, presurgical workup may be revisited.
