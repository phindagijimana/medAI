Assessment / Impression

This is an adult patient with focal epilepsy manifested by focal aware seizures, with onset at age 27. Etiology remains unknown. The patient currently reports seizure freedom, quantified as 0 seizures per month, on combination therapy with valproate and lacosamide.

The longitudinal epilepsy course is drug-resistant given the prior medication history, including inadequate seizure control with carbamazepine and intolerance of topiramate and oxcarbazepine.
