Assessment / Impression

This patient has focal epilepsy with focal to bilateral tonic-clonic seizures, with onset at age 11 and a reported frequency of approximately four seizures per month. The referral characterizes the etiology as structural, although the available MRI report is normal. Further characterization would require review of the original MRI and EEG data, if available. At present, there is insufficient information to localize the focal onset more precisely or to r/o whether the normal MRI reflects an occult structural abnormality not apparent on the existing study.
