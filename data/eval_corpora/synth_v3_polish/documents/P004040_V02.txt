MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Hx of generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 19, reportedly occurring approximately once weekly and drug responsive on carbamazepine. Structural etiology suspected; r/o epileptogenic lesion. Possible autoimmune contribution. No h/o CNS infection. Family hx notable for epilepsy in father.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner without IV contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, midline shift, or extra-axial collection.

Brain Parenchyma:  
Supratentorial: Small region of chronic encephalomalacia and surrounding gliosis in the right frontal lobe, with mild focal volume loss. No associated restricted diffusion, mass effect, or susceptibility artifact. No acute infarct, intracranial hemorrhage, or mass lesion.  
Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality or restricted diffusion.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Bilateral hippocampi symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal volume loss or abnormal T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. No mesial temporal sclerosis. No imaging evidence of active limbic encephalitis; a possible autoimmune contribution cannot be excluded by MRI alone.

Cortex: Normal gyral and sulcal pattern. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development identified.

White Matter: Aside from gliosis adjacent to the right frontal encephalomalacia, white matter signal is within normal limits for age. Corpus callosum normally formed.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal morphology and signal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature: Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids are maintained on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Small region of chronic right frontal encephalomalacia and gliosis, which may represent a structural seizure substrate in the appropriate electroclinical context.  
2. Normal appearance of the bilateral hippocampi. No mesial temporal sclerosis or visible malformation of cortical development.  
3. No acute intracranial abnormality. No MRI evidence of active limbic encephalitis; possible autoimmune contribution cannot be excluded by imaging alone.
