Assessment / Impression

This is an adult patient with focal epilepsy manifested by focal impaired-awareness seizures, with onset at age 56. The patient currently reports approximately five seizures per month. The epilepsy etiology remains unknown.

The MRI reportedly demonstrates nonspecific white matter changes without a specified causative lesion. Available data do not identify a structural etiology. Additional review of the actual MRI images, if obtainable, would be helpful to confirm the reported findings.

The patient is currently treated with lacosamide. Prior treatment included perampanel, discontinued for GI intolerance, and lamotrigine, discontinued because of inadequate seizure control. Based on the supplied classification, this is drug-responsive focal epilepsy rather than established drug-resistant epilepsy. The prior history does not document two tolerated, appropriately selected ASM regimens that both failed for lack of efficacy, because perampanel was stopped for intolerance.

At present, there is insufficient information to recommend a presurgical evaluation. The available MRI description is nonspecific, and EEG localization data were not supplied. Further characterization may be considered as part of routine epilepsy management, particularly if the reported seizure frequency persists despite an optimized and tolerated lacosamide regimen.

Recommendations / Plan

1. Continue Vimpat (lacosamide) pending confirmation of the current dose, frequency, adherence, and tolerability. I did not recommend a specific dose change because the present dose was not available for review.
