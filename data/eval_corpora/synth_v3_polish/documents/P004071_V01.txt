Assessment / Impression

The patient has focal epilepsy with focal aware seizures beginning at age 13. Current seizure burden remains high at approximately 5 seizures per week despite a three-drug regimen of perampanel, brivaracetam, and lamotrigine. Prior trials of oxcarbazepine and topiramate were stopped because of rash. This clinical course meets the documented designation of drug-resistant epilepsy.

The epilepsy has a structural etiology in the setting of an MRI-identified tumor. At present, the available documentation does not provide sufficient anatomic or electrophysiologic detail to determine whether the tumor and seizure onset zone are concordant. Presurgical evaluation is appropriate given the frequent ongoing seizures and drug resistance. The principal question is whether the patient may be a candidate for lesion-directed resection or another epilepsy surgery approach after adequate localization.

If resection is not feasible or if the epileptogenic zone overlaps eloquent cortex, neuromodulation may be considered. Potential options could include the NeuroPace RNS System, Medtronic DBS Percept, or LivaNova VNS SenTiva, depending on seizure localization and the results of a comprehensive multidisciplinary evaluation. No specific device recommendation can be made until the necessary diagnostic data are obtained.
