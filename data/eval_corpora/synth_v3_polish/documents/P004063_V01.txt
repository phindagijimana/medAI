Assessment / Impression

This patient has focal structural epilepsy, with focal seizures progressing to bilateral tonic-clonic activity. Seizure onset occurred at age 7. The structural lesion identified on MRI is hippocampal sclerosis.

The current ASM is lamotrigine. The supplied phenotype categorizes the epilepsy as drug-responsive. Carbamazepine was previously trialed but was discontinued because of rash. Carbamazepine should therefore remain separate from the active medication list.

There is an important point requiring clarification: the reported seizure frequency is approximately 6 per week, while the epilepsy is simultaneously described as drug-responsive. If 6 events per week represents the current frequency despite an adequate lamotrigine regimen, additional assessment of the treatment response and event characterization would be appropriate. If this instead reflects a pretreatment or historical frequency, the current degree of seizure control should be documented more precisely. At present, the available information does not establish drug-resistant epilepsy, and I would not label the patient as having DRE based on the supplied record alone.
