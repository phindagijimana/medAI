MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: H/o focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 28, previously occurring up to 7 times per week and currently drug responsive on carbamazepine and lacosamide. Structural etiology suspected; r/o epileptogenic lesion.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the temporal lobes, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, double inversion recovery imaging, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, midline shift, extra-axial collection, or cerebral volume loss.

Brain Parenchyma:

Supratentorial: Focal cortical thickening with blurring of the gray-white matter junction and subtle subcortical T2/FLAIR hyperintensity involving the right superior frontal gyrus. Mild associated abnormal gyral morphology. No discrete mass effect, restricted diffusion, hemorrhage, or susceptibility artifact.

Infratentorial: Normal signal and morphology. No focal lesion.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Bilateral hippocampi symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture and digitations. No hippocampal T2/FLAIR hyperintensity or volume loss. No mesial temporal sclerosis. Amygdalae and parahippocampal gyri unremarkable.

Cortex: Right superior frontal abnormality as described above, compatible with focal cortical dysplasia, Palmini type IIa. No additional cortical thickening, gray-white junction blurring, polymicrogyria, heterotopia, or schizencephaly.

White Matter: No additional focal or confluent white matter signal abnormality. Corpus callosum normally formed.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology bilaterally.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal. Cerebellar tonsils in normal position.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Expected intracranial vascular flow voids maintained.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow abnormality.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Focal cortical thickening, gray-white matter junction blurring, and subtle subcortical T2/FLAIR hyperintensity in the right superior frontal gyrus, consistent with focal cortical dysplasia, Palmini type IIa. This represents a potential structural epileptogenic focus.
2. Normal mesial temporal structures. No mesial temporal sclerosis.
3. No acute infarct, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or hydrocephalus.
