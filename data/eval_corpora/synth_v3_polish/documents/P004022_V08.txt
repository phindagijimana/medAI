Assessment / Impression

This patient has epilepsy beginning at age 7, with generalized tonic-clonic seizures reported at a frequency of approximately 5 per week. The current classification provided is generalized epilepsy with a structural etiology. The patient remains on carbamazepine and is described as drug responsive.

The MRI reportedly demonstrates focal cortical dysplasia, and the focal lesion is probably epileptogenic. However, focal cortical dysplasia is a focal structural abnormality, whereas the supplied epilepsy classification is generalized. This does not permit an automatic change in diagnosis. Review of the actual MRI and EEG data is needed to r/o discordance between the current syndrome label and the underlying electroclinical seizure onset.

The high reported seizure frequency also requires clarification in the context of the stated drug-responsive designation. At present, the information supplied does not establish drug-resistant epilepsy, and there is no documented hx of failed adequate trials of multiple appropriately selected ASMs. Thus, a presurgical or neuromodulation pathway is not indicated solely from the available information. The immediate priorities are diagnostic confirmation, medication reconciliation, and objective characterization of the epilepsy.
