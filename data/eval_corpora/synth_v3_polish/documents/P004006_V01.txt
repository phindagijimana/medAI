Assessment / Impression

The patient has drug-resistant focal epilepsy with focal aware seizures beginning at age 36 and continuing at a frequency of approximately 2 per month. The epilepsy has a structural etiology based on MRI evidence of focal cortical dysplasia. Seizures remain active despite current polytherapy with perampanel, carbamazepine, and valproate, as well as prior trials of lacosamide, zonisamide, and topiramate that were stopped because of adverse effects.

Because the patient has ongoing seizures despite multiple ASMs and has an identified structural lesion, I recommend a comprehensive presurgical epilepsy evaluation. The initial goal will be to confirm electroclinical onset with VEEG, determine whether the seizures localize concordantly with the focal cortical dysplasia, and r/o nonepileptic mimics or a broader epileptogenic network.

If the MRI lesion, seizure semiology, and EEG findings are concordant, focal resection or lesion-directed surgery may be considered. If resection is not feasible or the epileptogenic zone overlaps eloquent cortex, neuromodulation options may include the NeuroPace RNS System. Other palliative options, depending on the completed evaluation, could include the LivaNova VNS SenTiva or Medtronic DBS Percept system. No specific surgical or device recommendation can be made until phase I testing is complete.
