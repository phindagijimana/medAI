Neurology Progress Note

Chief Complaint: Follow-up combined generalized and focal epilepsy

History of Present Illness:  
This is a 10-year-old girl with hx of combined generalized and focal epilepsy of structural etiology, seizure onset at age 10, and generalized tonic-clonic seizures. Prior brain MRI demonstrated hippocampal sclerosis. She is currently treated with Topamax (topiramate) monotherapy. This history is carried forward from her initial community neurology consultation on 05/11/2024.

Since the last visit, she continues to have breakthrough generalized tonic-clonic seizures at an average frequency of approximately 2 per month per family’s seizure calendar. Family did not bring exact event dates today. Events remain convulsive and consistent with her established seizure type. No interval antiseizure medication change is documented. Overall, her epilepsy has been responsive to medication, although seizure control remains incomplete. No interval ED visit or hospitalization was reported.

Current concern is the persistence of breakthrough seizures despite adherence to topiramate. Medication tolerability and the importance of hydration were reviewed. She previously trialed Keppra (levetiracetam), which was discontinued because of mood side effects. Family continues to track events in a seizure diary. Seizure precautions and indications for emergency evaluation were reviewed.
