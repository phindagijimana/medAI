Neurology Progress Note

Chief Complaint: Follow-up drug-resistant generalized epilepsy

History of Present Illness:  
The patient is a 43-year-old man with h/o generalized epilepsy of unknown etiology, presenting predominantly with generalized tonic-clonic seizures. Seizure onset was at age 30. He remains on triple therapy with Keppra (levetiracetam), Briviact (brivaracetam), and Trileptal (oxcarbazepine). His epilepsy has been considered drug resistant given continued seizures despite multiple appropriate medication trials. He previously trialed Tegretol (carbamazepine), which was discontinued due to inadequate seizure control.

Since the prior visit, his reported seizure burden remains approximately one generalized tonic-clonic seizure per year. Per available seizure calendar, no more precise dated event information was provided today. Current medication regimen was reviewed; no medication changes were made before this visit. Prior documentation has also raised the possibility of another generalized seizure type. Atypical absence cannot be excluded, although this remains possible rather than established.

His principal concern remains the risk of recurrent convulsive seizures despite polytherapy. He denies a hx of developmental delay. Medication adherence and the importance of maintaining an accurate seizure diary were reviewed. No additional seizure semiology was established during today’s visit.
