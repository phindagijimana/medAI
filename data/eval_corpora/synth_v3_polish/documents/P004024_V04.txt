Medical Decision-Making (MDM):

Patient is now at neurologic baseline with reassuring exam and no clear infectious, metabolic, or toxic trigger identified. No Ativan (lorazepam) or IV antiseizure load indicated.

Differential included breakthrough seizure, syncope, TIA, and psychogenic non-epileptic event. Semiology and known epilepsy favor focal impaired-awareness seizure. Syncope less likely given no complete LOC or cardiovascular prodrome. TIA less likely without focal deficit. Low concern for meningitis, intracranial hemorrhage, or acute stroke. Observed in ED without recurrence; remained hemodynamically stable and ambulatory.

Assessment:  
1. Breakthrough focal impaired-awareness seizure.  
2. Drug-responsive focal epilepsy due to LGI1 antibody-mediated immune etiology.  
3. Incidental lesion on prior brain MRI.
