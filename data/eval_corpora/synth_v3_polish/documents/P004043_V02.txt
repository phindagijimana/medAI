Medical Decision-Making (MDM):
Presentation most consistent with breakthrough generalized tonic-clonic seizure in patient with established drug-resistant generalized epilepsy. She has returned to neurologic baseline and has no focal deficit, meningismus, persistent AMS, or evidence of status epilepticus. CT obtained to r/o acute hemorrhage given known vascular malformation and was negative for acute process. Metabolic and infectious evaluation unrevealing.

Differential considered included epileptic seizure, syncope, TIA, and psychogenic non-epileptic event. Witnessed generalized convulsions with post-ictal confusion and established epilepsy favor true seizure. No prodromal cardiopulmonary symptoms or EKG abnormality to suggest arrhythmic syncope; no persistent deficit to support TIA. No additional seizure occurred in the ED. Ativan (lorazepam) and IV antiseizure loading were therefore not indicated. Seizure precautions maintained.

Assessment:
1. Breakthrough generalized tonic-clonic seizure
2. Drug-resistant generalized epilepsy, structural etiology
3. Cerebral vascular malformation

Disposition:
Admit to neurology. Continue Tegretol, Fycompa, and Lamictal once home doses verified. Seizure precautions, no driving, no swimming/bathing alone, no heights or operating heavy machinery. Return/emergent escalation for seizure lasting >5 minutes, repeated seizures without recovery, injury, new focal deficit, severe headache, or persistent confusion.
