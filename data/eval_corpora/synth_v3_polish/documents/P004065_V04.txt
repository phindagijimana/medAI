This requires expedited epilepsy specialist reassessment rather than acute medication adjustment in the ED.

Assessment:  
1. Breakthrough focal impaired-awareness seizure.  
2. Focal epilepsy, immune/CASPR2-associated; probable drug-resistant course.

Disposition: Discharge home in stable condition after neurology evaluation. Continue levetiracetam exactly as prescribed; no driving. Reviewed seizure precautions, including no swimming alone, baths, heights, open flames, or operating machinery. Close outpatient epilepsy clinic follow-up advised. Return immediately for seizure lasting >5 minutes, repeated seizures without recovery, new focal deficit, persistent confusion, fever, injury, or other worsening symptoms.
