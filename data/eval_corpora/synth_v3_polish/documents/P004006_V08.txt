Medical Decision-Making (MDM):  
Presentation most consistent with breakthrough focal aware seizure in a patient with known structural, drug-resistant focal epilepsy. She has baseline events approximately twice monthly despite Fycompa, Tegretol, and Depakote. No ongoing seizure activity, clustering, status epilepticus, persistent encephalopathy, trauma, or focal neurologic deficit. Infectious and metabolic triggers were considered; ED workup is reassuring.

Differential included epileptic seizure vs syncope, TIA, and psychogenic nonepileptic event. Preserved awareness and stereotyped event matching prior seizures favor focal aware seizure. No cardiopulmonary prodrome or EKG abnormality to suggest syncope, and no persistent deficit concerning for TIA/CVA. No Ativan (lorazepam) or second-line IV ASM indicated. Observed in ED without recurrence and remained at baseline.

Assessment:  
1. Breakthrough focal aware seizure.  
2. Drug-resistant structural focal epilepsy due to focal cortical dysplasia.

Disposition:  
Discharge home in stable condition. Continue perampanel, carbamazepine, and valproate without missed doses or self-adjustment. Neurology/epilepsy clinic follow-up within 1–2 weeks. Seizure precautions reviewed: no driving per state law/neurology clearance, swimming alone, heights, open flames, or operating heavy machinery. Return immediately for seizure lasting >5 minutes, recurrent seizures without recovery, new weakness, severe headache, fever, injury, or persistent confusion.
