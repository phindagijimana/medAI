Indication: H/o drug-resistant generalized epilepsy with typical absence seizures, onset at age 30; r/o interictal epileptiform activity. Etiology unknown. Currently seizure-free, with 0 seizures/month.

Clinical History: The patient has generalized epilepsy manifested by typical absence seizures beginning at age 30. Although historically drug-resistant, the patient currently reports no breakthrough seizures. Current ASMs are brivaracetam and perampanel. Previously trialed levetiracetam, discontinued due to cognitive side effects. Family hx is notable for a sister with febrile seizures. Study obtained while on the current medication regimen.

Technique: A 30-minute digital EEG was performed using the international 10-20 system with 21 scalp electrodes and a single-channel ECG. Referential and bipolar montages were reviewed. Standard activation procedures included hyperventilation for 3 minutes and intermittent photic stimulation. Video was recorded concurrently. The patient was awake and drowsy during the study. Stage N2 sleep was not achieved.

Description:

Background: In the maximally alert state, there was a symmetric, well-organized 10 Hz posterior dominant rhythm of 30-60 µV that attenuated appropriately with eye opening. An expected anterior-to-posterior gradient was present. Low-amplitude beta activity was seen frontocentrally. No persistent focal slowing or interhemispheric asymmetry was identified.

Awake state: Normal organization and reactivity. No generalized or focal epileptiform discharges were present. Specifically, no generalized 3-Hz spike-and-wave or polyspike-and-wave activity was observed.

Drowsiness/sleep: Drowsiness was characterized by attenuation of the PDR, slow roving eye movements, and diffuse theta activity. Brief vertex transients were present. Sustained stage N2 sleep was not recorded.

Hyperventilation: HV produced mild, symmetric buildup of generalized theta slowing without epileptiform activation. No absence seizure was elicited.

Photic stimulation: PS at frequencies from 1 to 30 Hz produced symmetric photic driving, maximal over O1/O2. No photoparoxysmal response was seen.

Interictal findings: No focal or generalized IEDs. No LPDs, GPDs, TIRDA, or other periodic/rhythmic pattern.

Ictal events: No electrographic seizures or clinical events were captured. The patient remained In NAD throughout the recording.

ECG: Regular rhythm throughout the sampled tracing.

Impression:

1. Normal routine EEG during wakefulness and drowsiness. No focal slowing, interictal epileptiform discharges, or electrographic seizures were identified.
2. The absence of generalized spike-and-wave discharges on this recording does not exclude the clinical diagnosis of generalized epilepsy with typical absence seizures, particularly in a patient receiving brivaracetam and perampanel.
3. If diagnostic uncertainty persists or breakthrough staring episodes recur, prolonged ambulatory EEG or inpatient LTM/video-EEG may provide greater sampling sensitivity.
