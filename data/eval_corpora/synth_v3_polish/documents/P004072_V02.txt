Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner. Findings:

Brain Parenchyma:

Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality involving the brainstem or cerebellum.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Bilateral hippocampi symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture and normal morphology. No hippocampal T2/FLAIR hyperintensity or volume loss. Amygdalae and parahippocampal gyri unremarkable. No mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Gyral pattern and cortical thickness within normal limits. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white matter junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development identified.

White Matter: A few scattered punctate T2/FLAIR hyperintense foci are present in the bilateral frontal subcortical white matter. These are nonspecific in distribution and extent. No confluent white matter signal abnormality or lesion specifically suggestive of demyelination.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal volume and signal. Cerebellar tonsils in normal position.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Major intracranial vascular flow voids are preserved.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion. Sellar and suprasellar regions unremarkable.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Clear.

Orbits: No gross abnormality.

Impression:

1. Few punctate bilateral frontal subcortical white matter T2/FLAIR hyperintensities, nonspecific.
2. No structural epileptogenic lesion identified. Normal symmetric appearance of the hippocampi without evidence of mesial temporal sclerosis or malformation of cortical development.
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
