Assessment / Impression

This patient has focal epilepsy manifested by focal impaired-awareness seizures, with onset at age 75 and a reported frequency of approximately four seizures per week. MRI demonstrates hippocampal sclerosis, supporting a structural etiology. The epilepsy has been characterized as drug responsive, and the current regimen consists of carbamazepine and valproate.

The available information is not sufficient to determine whether the current event frequency reflects the period before the present regimen, persistent events despite partial medication benefit, or a more recent change in control. Confirming the medication doses and the temporal relationship between treatment and event frequency will therefore be important. Likewise, the submitted information does not include prior EEG or VEEG localization data.

Perampanel was previously discontinued because of rash and should remain clearly separated from the active medication list. There is no family hx of epilepsy.

1. Continue Tegretol (carbamazepine) and Depakote (valproate) for now. The exact doses and BID/TID schedule should be reconciled with the patient’s medication bottles, dispensing pharmacy, and referring neurology record before changes are made.
