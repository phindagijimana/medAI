MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Hx of generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 34, approximately 3/month. Reportedly drug responsive on valproate, although clinical concern for likely drug-resistant focal epilepsy. Evaluate structural etiology; r/o mesial temporal sclerosis. Family h/o febrile seizures in maternal aunt.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner without IV contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin-section coronal imaging through the temporal lobes, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, extra-axial collection, or midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma: No restricted diffusion to indicate acute infarction. No acute intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or susceptibility abnormality. No intracranial mass identified on this noncontrast examination.

Supratentorial: Preserved gray-white matter differentiation. No focal encephalomalacia or other parenchymal lesion.

Infratentorial: No focal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Subjective volume loss of the left hippocampus compared with the right, with left-right asymmetry of approximately 20%. Associated increased T2/FLAIR signal and blurring/loss of normal internal hippocampal architecture. Mild prominence of the adjacent left temporal horn. Right hippocampus is normal in volume and signal with preserved internal architecture. Findings are consistent with left mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Normal gyral and sulcal pattern. No cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: No significant white matter signal abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal. No cerebellar volume loss.

Vasculature: Expected flow voids maintained on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Left hippocampal volume loss, T2/FLAIR hyperintensity, and loss of internal architecture, consistent with left mesial temporal sclerosis and a probable structural epileptogenic substrate.
2. No additional epileptogenic lesion or malformation of cortical development identified.
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
