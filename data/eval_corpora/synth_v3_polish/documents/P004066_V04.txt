A major objective of this admission is to capture representative events with family or patient confirmation and define the ictal onset pattern and any reproducible lateralizing signs.

Exact dates of the most recent events and specific triggers are not documented in the materials available today. Given the relatively low event frequency, medication reduction and activation procedures may be required to improve diagnostic yield, balanced against the risk of seizure clustering or focal to bilateral tonic-clonic activity.

Prior medication trials include Lamictal (lamotrigine), discontinued because of cognitive side effects; Trileptal (oxcarbazepine), discontinued because of inadequate seizure control; and Depakote (valproate), discontinued because of mood side effects. He has continued to have seizures on the current three-drug regimen. This pattern is consistent with DRE rather than medication intolerance alone.

Epilepsy has previously been categorized as focal epilepsy of structural etiology, although the available recent brain MRI was read as normal, without an identified epileptogenic lesion or MTS. Thus, this is currently a nonlesional presurgical evaluation from an imaging standpoint. A prior EEG was obtained in the VA setting, but the detailed tracing and formal findings are not available in the admission materials. There is no documented prior epilepsy surgery or implanted neuromodulation device. Following completion of the presurgical evaluation, his data may be reviewed at multidisciplinary patient review conference (PRC).
