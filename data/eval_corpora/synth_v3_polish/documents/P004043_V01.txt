Neurology Progress Note

Chief Complaint: Follow-up drug-resistant generalized epilepsy

History of Present Illness:  
The patient is a 32-year-old woman with h/o structural, drug-resistant generalized epilepsy characterized by generalized tonic-clonic seizures. Per prior notes, seizure onset was at age 29. Brain MRI demonstrated a vascular malformation felt to represent the structural etiology. Current ASMs are Tegretol (carbamazepine), Fycompa (perampanel), and Lamictal (lamotrigine). She denies h/o developmental delay.

She continues to have frequent generalized tonic-clonic seizures despite the above multidrug regimen. Seizure diary reviewed today; current frequency averages approximately six seizures/week. There have been no documented medication changes since the prior regimen was established. She previously trialed Vimpat (lacosamide), which was discontinued due to cognitive side effects. Current medication adherence, dosing schedule, and seizure calendar were reviewed in clinic.

Her primary concern remains the high seizure burden and associated effect on day-to-day safety and quality of life. We discussed that ongoing seizures despite adequate trials of multiple ASMs are consistent with drug-resistant epilepsy. Reviewed seizure precautions, medication adherence, and the importance of continued diary documentation.
