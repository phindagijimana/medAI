In addition, aspects of the reported spell history have been described as possibly suggestive of a PNES component. Epileptic seizures and nonepileptic events may coexist; therefore, capture of each habitual event type with VEEG is important before any definitive surgical recommendation. No interval events have been documented since the most recent available follow-up.
