MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: H/o structural focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures beginning at age 33, currently approximately 7/week; drug-responsive on brivaracetam and valproate. Epilepsy protocol MRI, r/o mesial temporal sclerosis.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin-section coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, DIR, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE. No IV contrast administered.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, midline shift, extra-axial collection, or abnormal mass effect.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarct, intracranial hemorrhage, or mass lesion. No restricted diffusion or pathologic susceptibility artifact.

Supratentorial: Normal cerebral volume for age. No focal encephalomalacia or abnormal cortical/subcortical signal outside the mesial temporal abnormality described below.

Infratentorial: Brainstem and cerebellum normal in signal and morphology.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Asymmetric hippocampal volume loss, approximately 20%, with corresponding T2/FLAIR hyperintensity and loss of normal internal hippocampal architecture, consistent with hippocampal sclerosis. Mild associated dilatation of the ipsilateral temporal horn. The contralateral hippocampus is preserved in volume and signal, with maintained internal architecture.

Cortex: Normal gyral and sulcal pattern. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, polymicrogyria, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: No significant white matter signal abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: No focal abnormality. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature: Major intracranial vascular flow voids preserved on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Hippocampal sclerosis, characterized by asymmetric hippocampal volume loss, T2/FLAIR hyperintensity, and loss of internal architecture.  
2. No additional structural epileptogenic lesion or malformation of cortical development identified.  
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.  
4. If progression is demonstrated on follow-up MRI, PET and/or SPECT may be obtained to assess concordance.
