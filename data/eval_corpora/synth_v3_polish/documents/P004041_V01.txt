Assessment / Impression

This patient has focal epilepsy of genetic etiology, presenting with focal to bilateral tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 76. The reported seizure frequency is four per week. Current treatment consists of zonisamide and valproate.

Based on the information submitted, this is categorized as drug-responsive epilepsy and not drug-resistant epilepsy. Nevertheless, the reported ongoing frequency is clinically significant and requires careful confirmation. In particular, we need to establish whether four seizures per week is the current frequency while taking both ASMs, a historical pretreatment frequency, or an estimate that includes other event types. Exact medication doses and frequencies also need to be verified.

At present, there is insufficient medication and diagnostic documentation to make a safe dose change or to determine whether additional EEG or imaging review would alter management. There is likewise insufficient information to consider a presurgical evaluation, and the supplied classification does not identify the patient as having DRE.
