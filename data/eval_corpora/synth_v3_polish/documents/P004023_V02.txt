MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: Hx of focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures, onset at age 75, currently reported approximately 4/week. Suspected structural etiology; r/o epileptogenic lesion. Seizures described as drug responsive on carbamazepine and valproate. Previously trialed perampanel, discontinued due to rash. No h/o family history of epilepsy.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin-section coronal imaging through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Mild generalized parenchymal volume loss with proportional prominence of the ventricles and sulci. No hydrocephalus or extra-axial collection. No midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma: No acute infarct, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or edema. No restricted diffusion or abnormal susceptibility artifact.

Supratentorial: No focal intra-axial mass. No prior territorial cortical infarct.

Infratentorial: No focal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Asymmetric hippocampal volume loss, estimated at approximately 20% side-to-side difference, with associated T2/FLAIR hyperintensity and loss of normal internal architecture. Mild prominence of the ipsilateral temporal horn. Findings are consistent with hippocampal sclerosis. No mesial temporal mass.

Cortex: Normal gyral and sulcal pattern. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: Minimal scattered supratentorial T2/FLAIR hyperintensities, nonspecific and likely reflecting chronic small-vessel ischemic change.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal signal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Major intracranial vascular flow voids are maintained.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered paranasal sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells are clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Hippocampal sclerosis, with asymmetric hippocampal volume loss, T2/FLAIR hyperintensity, and loss of internal architecture.
2. No acute intracranial abnormality or additional structural epileptogenic lesion identified.
3. If subsequent MRI demonstrates progression, PET/SPECT may be obtained to assess concordance. Should seizure recurrence occur, reconsideration of presurgical workup is recommended.
