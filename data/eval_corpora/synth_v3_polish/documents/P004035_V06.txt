Chief Complaint: Follow-up drug-resistant epilepsy

History of Present Illness:

This is a 38-year-old man with hx of drug-resistant generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures, onset at age 15, in the setting of GAD65 antibody-associated immune etiology. Seizures have previously been classified as generalized, although the available semiology is possibly suggestive of focal onset seizures. Current ASMs are Lamictal (lamotrigine), Tegretol (carbamazepine), and Vimpat (lacosamide).

He was last seen on 03/04/2025. Seizure calendar reviewed today; he continues to average approximately 7 generalized tonic-clonic seizures per week. Individual seizure dates were not consistently recorded. There are no additional interval events or medication changes listed in the available record. He remains on the same three-drug regimen. Briviact (brivaracetam) was previously trialed and discontinued due to inadequate seizure control.

His primary concern remains the high ongoing seizure burden despite multiple appropriately selected ASMs. Given the possibility of focal onset, further electroclinical characterization would be helpful before making additional treatment decisions. He was encouraged to continue maintaining a detailed seizure diary, including date, time, duration, possible warning symptoms, recovery period, and any missed medication doses.
