This is a 28-year-old man with h/o drug-resistant generalized epilepsy characterized by generalized tonic-clonic seizures. Seizure onset was at age 26. His epilepsy is considered structurally associated given MRI evidence of focal cortical dysplasia, although his electroclinical seizure type is generalized. Prior multidisciplinary evaluation determined that he was not an appropriate candidate for focal resection based on the available generalized seizure pattern. Neuromodulation options were reviewed, and he elected implantation of a LivaNova VNS SenTiva system.

Prior medication trials included lamotrigine, discontinued because of sedation; oxcarbazepine and lacosamide, discontinued because of inadequate seizure control; and topiramate and carbamazepine, discontinued because of mood side effects. His current regimen consists of perampanel, levetiracetam, and brivaracetam. The concurrent antiseizure medications were reviewed with the Epilepsy service and were continued without change during this brief surgical admission.
