Chief Complaint: Follow-up focal epilepsy

History of Present Illness:  
This is a 78-year-old woman with hx of focal epilepsy with focal to bilateral tonic-clonic seizures, onset at age 60. Epilepsy is attributed to a remote infectious CNS insult. Prior findings were probably consistent with mesial temporal sclerosis, although lateralization has not been established. Her epilepsy has met criteria for drug resistance based on prior medication failures. She remains on dual therapy with Depakote (valproate) and Tegretol (carbamazepine).

Previously trialed medications include Trileptal (oxcarbazepine), discontinued due to mood side effects; Vimpat (lacosamide), discontinued due to sedation; and Lamictal (lamotrigine), discontinued due to rash.

She presents for routine follow-up and ongoing medication monitoring. Current priority is maintaining seizure control while limiting cumulative adverse effects and drug interactions from dual ASM therapy. Medication adherence and continued use of a seizure calendar were reviewed.
