Neurology Progress Note

Chief Complaint: Follow-up drug-resistant focal epilepsy

History of Present Illness:

This is a 63-year-old man with hx of DEPDC5-related genetic focal epilepsy with focal aware seizures beginning at age 37. Per the prior note, his epilepsy has remained drug-resistant despite multiple appropriate antiseizure medication trials. Current regimen is Keppra (levetiracetam), Topamax (topiramate), Fycompa (perampanel), and Tegretol (carbamazepine). Genetic testing identified a causative DEPDC5 c.4396C>T variant.

Since his last visit on 10/09/2025, he continues to have approximately five focal aware seizures per month per seizure calendar. He retains awareness during his typical events and specifically denies post-ictal confusion. No interval medication changes are documented. His current seizure burden remains consistent with drug-resistant epilepsy despite four concurrent ASMs.

The primary concern today is continued breakthrough seizure activity. Some events may have a different semiology; atypical absence is possible and cannot be excluded based on the available clinical description. We discussed the importance of continuing the seizure diary and documenting duration, retained responsiveness, motor features, and recovery after each event.
