Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner without IV contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and thin-section coronal FLAIR through the temporal lobes, DIR, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

  - Supratentorial: Approximately 6 mm rounded lesion at the cortical-subcortical junction of the left middle frontal gyrus demonstrating heterogeneous T2 signal and prominent susceptibility blooming, with a thin complete hemosiderin rim. No surrounding edema or mass effect. Appearance is most consistent with a small cavernous malformation. No acute infarct or restricted diffusion.
  - Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality.

- Mesial Temporal Structures: Bilateral hippocampi symmetric in size and signal, with preserved internal architecture. No hippocampal volume loss or T2/FLAIR hyperintensity. No mesial temporal sclerosis.

- Cortex: Normal cortical thickness and gyral pattern. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle sign, polymicrogyria, or heterotopia.

- White Matter: No additional significant white matter signal abnormality.

- Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

- Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal. No cerebellar atrophy or tonsillar ectopia.

- Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids maintained.

- Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

- Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Minimal scattered paranasal sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells clear.

- Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Small left middle frontal gyrus cavernous malformation without edema or evidence of recent hemorrhage. Although potentially incidental, this focal MRI lesion is likely epileptogenic in the appropriate electroclinical setting and may support the clinical concern for probable focal epilepsy.
2. Normal and symmetric mesial temporal structures. No mesial temporal sclerosis or malformation of cortical development.
3. No acute intracranial abnormality.
