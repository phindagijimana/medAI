Assessment / Impression

This patient has genetic, drug-resistant combined generalized and focal epilepsy associated with the causative SCN1A c.5734C>T variant. The principal reported seizure type is generalized tonic-clonic, with a high current burden of approximately 7 seizures per week. Seizures remain uncontrolled despite concurrent therapy with four antiseizure medications and prior trials of two additional agents that were stopped because of adverse effects.

The immediate priority is to confirm the complete medication regimen, characterize the electroclinical seizure pattern, and determine whether all reported events are captured by the current epilepsy classification. Because no prior EEG, LTM, or MRI data were available for review, there is not yet sufficient information to establish a focal surgical hypothesis or determine candidacy for resective therapy.

Given the drug-resistant course, comprehensive epilepsy center evaluation is appropriate. In addition to medication optimization, neuromodulation may ultimately be relevant. Options discussed in the broader DRE framework include the LivaNova VNS SenTiva, NeuroPace RNS System, and Medtronic DBS Percept. Selection among these therapies requires confirmation of seizure onset and network characteristics through VEEG and review of imaging; no specific device recommendation is made today.

1. Complete medication reconciliation, including exact doses, formulations, and BID/TID/QID schedules for carbamazepine, valproate, lamotrigine, and lacosamide. No medication change should be made until this information is confirmed.
