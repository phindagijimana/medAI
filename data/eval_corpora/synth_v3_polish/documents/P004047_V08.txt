MRI Brain — Epilepsy Protocol

Exam Date: xx/xx/20xx  
Ordering Provider: Dr. xxx

Indication: H/o structural focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures, onset at age 33 and reported frequency approximately 7/week. Clinically characterized as drug responsive; currently on brivaracetam and valproate. Epilepsy-protocol MRI to evaluate for structural seizure focus and r/o mesial temporal sclerosis.

Comparison: None.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner without IV contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal T2/FLAIR images through the mesial temporal structures, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, extra-axial collection, or midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma: No restricted diffusion to suggest acute infarction. No acute hemorrhage, mass effect, or abnormal susceptibility deposition. Supratentorial and infratentorial parenchymal volumes otherwise preserved.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Left hippocampal volume subjectively decreased relative to the right, with left-right asymmetry of approximately 20%. Associated increased T2/FLAIR signal and loss of normal internal hippocampal architecture. Mild prominence of the left temporal horn. Right hippocampus normal in volume and signal with preserved internal architecture. Findings consistent with left hippocampal sclerosis.

Cortex: Normal gyral and sulcal pattern. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, polymicrogyria, or other malformation of cortical development identified.

White Matter: No significant white matter signal abnormality. Corpus callosum normally formed.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal signal and volume. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature: Expected major intracranial vascular flow voids maintained on conventional sequences.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Left mesial temporal sclerosis, manifested by approximately 20% left hippocampal volume loss, increased T2/FLAIR signal, and loss of internal architecture.
2. No additional structural epileptogenic lesion identified. No acute intracranial hemorrhage, infarction, mass, or midline shift.
3. If subsequent MRI demonstrates progression, consider interictal FDG-PET and/or ictal/interictal SPECT for concordance with the suspected seizure focus.
