Assessment / Impression

This is an adult patient with focal epilepsy manifested by focal impaired-awareness seizures, with seizure onset at age 24. The current event burden is reported as approximately 2 seizures per week. Etiology is unknown based on the information available today.

The referral phenotype classifies the epilepsy as drug responsive. The patient is currently treated with brivaracetam and perampanel, although the exact doses and schedules require confirmation. Prior lamotrigine was discontinued because of cognitive side effects, and prior carbamazepine was discontinued because of sedation. These adverse effects should be considered when reviewing future treatment options.

At this time, the major limitation is the absence of detailed semiology, verified medication dosing, and primary EEG and MRI data. I would not assign a more specific focal epilepsy localization or structural etiology without those records. There is also insufficient information in the supplied documentation to characterize the magnitude of medication response beyond the provided designation of drug-responsive epilepsy.
