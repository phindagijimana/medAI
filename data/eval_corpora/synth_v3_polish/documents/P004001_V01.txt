Assessment / Impression

This patient has epilepsy beginning at age 8, currently classified as generalized epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures. The epilepsy is considered drug resistant given the prior failure of four ASMs and the current requirement for three-drug therapy. Despite this historical DRE designation, the current documented seizure burden is 0 seizures per month.

The suspected etiology is structural, based on MRI evidence of hippocampal sclerosis. There is some electroclinical uncertainty. Hippocampal sclerosis would be more typical of focal temporal lobe epilepsy, and the convulsive events may therefore represent possibly focal onset seizures progressing to bilateral tonic-clonic activity. Conversely, the existing diagnosis remains generalized epilepsy because the available EEG and detailed semiology are insufficient to reclassify it.

The records also describe findings suggestive of a possible PNES component. This is not established. If the patient develops recurrent events, VEEG capture would be important to characterize the spells, r/o nonepileptic events, and determine whether there is a consistent focal onset.
