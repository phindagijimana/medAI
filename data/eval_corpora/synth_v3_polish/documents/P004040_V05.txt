Neurology Progress Note

Chief Complaint: Follow-up epilepsy with generalized tonic-clonic seizures

History of Present Illness:

The patient is a 40-year-old woman with epilepsy characterized by generalized tonic-clonic seizures beginning at age 19. Her epilepsy has been considered structural in etiology given encephalomalacia on prior brain MRI. She is currently treated with Tegretol (carbamazepine) and has demonstrated a meaningful medication response; she is therefore considered drug-responsive, although she is not presently seizure-free. A possible autoimmune contribution has also been raised, but this remains uncertain. She denies any hx of CNS infection.

She was last seen 05/26/2025. Since that visit, there have been no documented medication changes or interval hospital encounters. She continues to describe convulsive seizures approximately once per week. Her seizure calendar was reviewed and remains consistent with an average frequency of one generalized tonic-clonic seizure weekly, without a clear sustained change since the prior visit. She continues carbamazepine as prescribed.

Her principal concern remains the ongoing frequency and unpredictability of the convulsions, with associated effects on safety and day-to-day independence. We reviewed the importance of maintaining a written seizure diary, taking medication at consistent times, and notifying the clinic promptly for an increase in seizure frequency or severity.
