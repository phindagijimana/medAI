Indication:  Drug-resistant focal epilepsy with focal impaired-awareness seizures, onset at age 33 and occurring approximately 2/year. Structural etiology suspected; r/o epileptogenic lesion and mesial temporal sclerosis. Current ASMs: topiramate and brivaracetam. Previously trialed lacosamide, discontinued for inadequate seizure control, and zonisamide, discontinued for cognitive side effects. No h/o photosensitivity.

Comparison:  None.

Technique:  Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner without intravenous contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal T2/FLAIR images through the temporal lobes, double inversion recovery, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus, extra-axial collection, or midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma:

Supratentorial: Focal cortical thickening and blurring of the gray-white junction involving the left posterior superior frontal gyrus, with a subtle radially oriented T2/FLAIR hyperintense transmantle band extending toward the superolateral margin of the left lateral ventricle. No associated mass effect, restricted diffusion, or susceptibility artifact. Imaging appearance is consistent with focal cortical dysplasia, most compatible with Palmini type IIa.

Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Mild asymmetric volume loss of the left hippocampus compared with the right, with left-right volume asymmetry subjectively approximately 15-20%. Subtle increased T2/FLAIR signal and partial loss of internal hippocampal architecture are present on the left. Findings are probably consistent with left mesial temporal sclerosis. Right hippocampus normal in volume and signal with preserved internal architecture.

Cortex: Left superior frontal cortical abnormality as above. No additional cortical malformation, polymicrogyria, heterotopia, or schizencephaly.

White Matter: No additional significant white matter signal abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal. No cerebellar atrophy.

Vasculature (as seen on conventional sequences): Preserved major intracranial vascular flow voids.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid sinus mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Focal cortical dysplasia involving the left posterior superior frontal gyrus, with cortical thickening, gray-white junction blurring, and a subtle transmantle sign; imaging phenotype most compatible with Palmini type IIa.
2. Mild left hippocampal volume loss, T2/FLAIR hyperintensity, and partial loss of internal architecture, probably consistent with left mesial temporal sclerosis.
3. No acute infarct, intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or hydrocephalus.
