Neurology Progress Note

Chief Complaint: Follow-up generalized epilepsy with myoclonic seizures

History of Present Illness:

The patient is a 27-year-old man with a hx of generalized epilepsy characterized by myoclonic seizures, onset at age 9, in the setting of a structural lesion consistent with focal cortical dysplasia on prior brain MRI. He remains on Depakote (valproate) 500 mg BID. His epilepsy has been drug responsive, although he continues to have intermittent breakthrough myoclonic events.

He was last seen on 10/31/2024 for an initial consultation in community neurology. Since that visit, he reports an average seizure frequency of approximately one myoclonic seizure per month. His seizure calendar was reviewed, although exact dates were not recorded. There have been no medication changes since the prior visit. He reports taking valproate as prescribed. No interval ED evaluation, hospitalization, or injury related to seizures. He denies urinary incontinence with his events. He also has no hx of febrile seizures in childhood.

The ongoing monthly myoclonic seizures remain his principal epilepsy-related concern. He does not report a new seizure semiology or prolonged event. Prior ASM trials included Fycompa (perampanel), discontinued due to rash, and Zonegran (zonisamide), discontinued because of cognitive side effects. He has not restarted either medication.
