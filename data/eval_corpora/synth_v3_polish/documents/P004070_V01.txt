Assessment / Impression

This patient has focal epilepsy characterized by focal impaired awareness seizures, with onset at age 6 and a current reported frequency of approximately one seizure per year. The underlying etiology has been categorized as metabolic in the available records.

Given ongoing seizures on lacosamide and carbamazepine, together with prior unsuccessful trials of perampanel, topiramate, and zonisamide, this is likely drug-resistant focal epilepsy. Perampanel was limited by mood side effects, topiramate by sedation, and zonisamide by inadequate seizure control.

The reported MRI finding is probably consistent with mesial temporal sclerosis, but this remains to be confirmed through direct image review or repeat epilepsy-protocol MRI. At present, the available information is insufficient to establish electroclinical concordance or determine whether there is a single resectable seizure-onset zone. A structured presurgical evaluation is reasonable to r/o a surgically remediable temporal focus.
