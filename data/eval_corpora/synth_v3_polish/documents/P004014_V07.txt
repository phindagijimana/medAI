Assessment / Impression

This is an adult patient with focal epilepsy beginning at age 24. The documented seizure type is focal impaired-awareness seizure, currently occurring approximately twice per week. Etiology is unknown.

The current ASMs are brivaracetam and perampanel. Prior lamotrigine was discontinued because of cognitive side effects, and prior carbamazepine was discontinued because of sedation. On the information available, these prior medication changes appear to have been driven by adverse effects rather than definite treatment failure. The epilepsy is therefore characterized as drug-responsive, and the present record does not establish DRE.

Additional diagnostic documentation is needed to better define the seizure semiology and electroclinical localization and to r/o an identifiable structural cause. Clarification of the exact brivaracetam and perampanel doses is also necessary before recommending a medication adjustment. At present, there is insufficient information to support a presurgical epilepsy assessment or neuromodulation discussion.

1. Continue Briviact (brivaracetam) and Fycompa (perampanel) at the currently prescribed doses pending complete medication reconciliation. The patient should not stop either ASM abruptly.
