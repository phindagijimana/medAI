Assessment / Impression

This is an XX-year-old patient with focal epilepsy beginning at age 11. The reported habitual seizure type is focal impaired awareness, occurring approximately 5 times per week. The patient denies associated loss of consciousness. The suspected etiology is immune-mediated epilepsy related to CASPR2 antibodies.

The patient is currently treated with levetiracetam and has been described as drug responsive.
