Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the brain performed on a 3T scanner without IV contrast. Sequences included sagittal T1, axial and coronal T2, axial and coronal FLAIR with thin coronal imaging through the temporal lobes, DIR, axial DWI/ADC, axial SWI, and 3D T1 MPRAGE.

Findings:

Ventricles and Extra-axial Spaces: Ventricles normal in size and configuration. No hydrocephalus or extra-axial collection. No midline shift.

Brain Parenchyma: No restricted diffusion to indicate acute infarction. No acute intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or vasogenic edema.

Supratentorial: Approximately 9 mm well-circumscribed lesion in the left temporal lobe demonstrating heterogeneous T2 signal and prominent blooming susceptibility, compatible with a small vascular malformation, most likely a cavernous malformation. No surrounding edema or evidence of recent hemorrhage.

Infratentorial: No focal signal abnormality.

Mesial Temporal Structures: Left hippocampal volume is subjectively decreased relative to the right, with left-right asymmetry of approximately 20%. Subtle increased T2/FLAIR signal and partial loss of normal internal architecture involving the left hippocampal body and tail. Right hippocampus normal in volume and signal with preserved internal architecture. Findings are consistent with probable left mesial temporal sclerosis.

Cortex: Normal gyral and sulcal pattern. No focal cortical thickening, blurring of the gray-white junction, transmantle sign, heterotopia, or other malformation of cortical development.

White Matter: No significant white matter signal abnormality.

Basal Ganglia and Thalami: Normal signal and morphology.

Brainstem and Cerebellum: Normal. No cerebellar tonsillar ectopia.

Vasculature: Major intracranial arterial flow voids are maintained. Left temporal vascular malformation as described above.

Skull Base and Calvarium: No focal marrow-replacing lesion.

Paranasal Sinuses and Mastoid Air Cells: Mild scattered ethmoid mucosal thickening. Mastoid air cells clear.

Orbits: Unremarkable.

Impression:

1. Findings consistent with probable left mesial temporal sclerosis, including approximately 20% relative left hippocampal volume loss, subtle T2/FLAIR hyperintensity, and partial loss of internal architecture.
2. Small left temporal lobe vascular malformation, most compatible with a cavernous malformation. No associated edema or recent hemorrhage.
3. No acute infarction, acute intracranial hemorrhage, mass effect, or hydrocephalus.
