EEG REPORT

Patient ID: E008  
Study Type: Routine EEG  
Clinical Indication: Evaluation for possible seizures.

Technical Description:  
A routine digital EEG was recorded using the international 10-20 electrode placement system with additional ECG monitoring. Recording included awake and drowsy states. Hyperventilation and intermittent photic stimulation were performed.

Findings:  
The posterior dominant rhythm was poorly sustained at 8 Hz and reactive to eye opening. Intermittent polymorphic delta-theta slowing was present over the left temporal region, maximal at T3/T5, with occasional spread to the left frontotemporal electrodes. No definite epileptiform discharges were identified. No electrographic seizures were recorded.

Hyperventilation did not produce additional abnormalities. Photic stimulation did not elicit a photoparoxysmal response.

Impression:  
Abnormal EEG due to intermittent left temporal focal slowing, suggesting focal cerebral dysfunction in this region. No epileptiform discharges or electrographic seizures were captured during this recording.
