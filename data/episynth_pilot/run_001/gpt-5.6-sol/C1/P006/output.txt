ELECTROENCEPHALOGRAM REPORT

Clinical Indication:
Evaluation of episodic altered awareness.

Technique:
Routine EEG recorded using the international 10–20 electrode placement system. The study included wakefulness and drowsiness. Photic stimulation and hyperventilation were performed.

Findings:
The waking background demonstrates a posterior dominant rhythm of 8 Hz that is symmetric and reactive to eye opening. Intermittent generalized theta slowing is present, with occasional brief runs of higher-amplitude delta activity. No focal slowing is identified.

Photic stimulation produces a symmetric driving response. Hyperventilation does not elicit additional abnormalities. No epileptiform discharges or electrographic seizures are recorded.

Impression:
Abnormal EEG due to intermittent generalized slowing, consistent with mild diffuse cerebral dysfunction. No epileptiform abnormalities or seizures are captured.
