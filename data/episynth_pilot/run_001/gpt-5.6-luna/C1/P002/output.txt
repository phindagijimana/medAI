Thank you for referring this patient for epilepsy review.

The current clinical information is consistent with **generalized epilepsy**. Seizures are occurring approximately **once per week**. No further seizure semiology, precipitating factors, medication history, or investigation results were provided for this review.

The ongoing weekly seizure frequency indicates that seizure control is not yet adequate. A detailed review of seizure type, adherence, potential triggers, antiseizure medication history, and relevant investigations—including EEG and brain imaging where clinically indicated—is recommended.

The patient should continue standard seizure-safety precautions, including avoiding driving in accordance with local regulations and taking care around bathing, swimming, heights, and open flames. They should seek urgent medical attention for a seizure lasting five minutes or longer, repeated seizures without recovery, serious injury, or breathing difficulty.

Follow-up with the epilepsy service is recommended to confirm the generalized epilepsy syndrome and guide treatment adjustment.
